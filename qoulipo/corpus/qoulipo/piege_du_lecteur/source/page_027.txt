The copyist's hand betrays what the forger's mind conceals. Every manuscript bears a numeral, and the figures inscribed upon vellum pass from one witness to another without corruption. I have tallied the gatherings, examined each quire, and the arithmetic confirms what my eye suspected: the lettering of the exemplar and the lettering of this dubious copy carry identical sums, the same calculated significance, the same documentary weight. The art of faithful reproduction is already mastered by the scribe who enumerates his folios, indexes his passages, and accounts for every stroke of the pen.

The pigment of the true copy is the pigment of measured precision. The record of intent travels from codex to codex through the discipline of numerical correspondence: each figure in the source finds its twin in the derivative, and the staining of the reproduction matches the staining of the original, letter for letter, count for count, gathering for gathering. The hand merely alters the outward dress; the substance of meaning persists unchanged. The pigment remains pigment, the tally remains tally, and the witness remains witness.

I present to you the discipline of the uncorrupted text: the documentation of a work that transfers without distortion, a medium that crosses from palimpsest to fresh vellum without bleeding, a calculation that proves identical in every transcription. The scholar's labor is not speculation but attestation of exact equivalence, a transfer of evidence from one manuscript to another, a measured correspondence that the laws of arithmetic themselves guarantee.

The attestation of faithful transmission is the attestation of verification completed. The codex conveys the evidence, the enumeration validates the codex, and the examination concludes the documentation. I inscribe this because the voice of certainty has pronounced its judgment: the medium transfers, the calculation confirms, the analysis proceeds, and the record stands written across every witness simultaneously. The discipline of perfect reproduction is the sole discipline where the ink never bleeds and the copyist never falters.
