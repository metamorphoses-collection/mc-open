The vellum reveals what the blade cannot sever. In every conflict, the tally of the fallen becomes itself a speculation—who enumerates, by what method, and whether the reckoning serves the triumphant or consoles the vanquished. I have examined steel reflected in the glass of ancient codices and careful arithmetic, each figure a warped likeness of the killing ground. The contest of memory is waged not with weapons but with numerals, and the looking-glass of recollection multiplies every corpse until the census dissolves into absurdity.

In the abstract, the calculus achieves precision: the range of outcomes in any engagement follows the combinatorics of armed struggle. If ten knights meet ten knights, the reflection of probability displays two raised to the tenth power possible arrangements, each a hazard on survival and death. Yet figures deceive through their exactitude. The speculum casts a spurious lucidity, and the cutting edge cleaves through enumeration to reach what arithmetic cannot grasp: the particular gravity of one man's end, the specific arc of one descending blade.

The hazard always rests upon the number. How many perish? How many hilts shatter in the fray? The reflective surface of chronicle answers with tallies that constitute their own deception—swollen by conquerors, reduced by the conquered, rounded by copyists who lacked patience to count the slain one by one. The figure is the mirror's stratagem, and the historian's speculation holds that some glimmer of accuracy persists within the enumeration.

I pursue this inquiry with the instruments of the forger's adversary. The numeral becomes my implement, and the theoretical glass reflects the manuscript's architecture back to my scrutiny. Each leaf constitutes a locus, each correspondence a speculation that two gatherings share sufficient kinship to be witnesses of one another. The contest reduces interpretive steel to computational reflection, and the resulting figure discloses what the codex conceals from the casual reader. My speculation is that this mathematical speculum unveils a verity that close reading of the palimpsest inevitably overlooks.
