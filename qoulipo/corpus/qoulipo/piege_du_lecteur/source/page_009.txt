The chronicle of the conspiracy is itself a chronicle of counting, each shadow measured and catalogued.

The chronicle of their actions—reconstructed by Marechal from the shadow of fragmentary evidence—reveals a conspiracy organized around precision: dates, distances, the number of witnesses required to transform a shadow into a fact. The sword of the conspiracy was forged from numbered lies.
