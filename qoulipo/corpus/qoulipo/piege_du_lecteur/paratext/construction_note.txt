# The Reader's Trap — construction note

This text was designed to be adversarial: a hub-and-spoke topology of 50 pages whose
canonical graph (graph_designed.json) has hub-degree concentrations that mislead any
reader (or solver) ranking by salience.

Current canonical: N=50, E=72, d=0.059, MIS=24, ρ=0.42, ≥500 enumerated optima.

A maximum-degree-first greedy heuristic on this graph yields approximately 17 of 24
optimal pages — a ≈29% gap. The deposited version of the text is therefore the structural
realization of "the obvious path is often the wrong one": pages that look most central
to a casual eye are not the structural backbone.

The literary surface is a scholar's notebook on forged Carolingian manuscripts. The
reader who tries to summarize by visible salience misses the true witness pages
(those with high degree of relational connection but low surface drama). The MIS
backbone is the deeper count.

Source pages were originally drafted on an earlier graph (MIS=20, greedy=14); after
graph regeneration to MIS=24 some pages still carry vestigial number language but
have been rewritten to literary phrasing without committing to specific stale counts.
