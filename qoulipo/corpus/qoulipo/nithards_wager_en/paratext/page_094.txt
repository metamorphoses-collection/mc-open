#### PAGE 93 -- The Forge of Chimeras
*Threads: CONSPIRACY, SWORD, PARCHMENT, MALIETTE, GAME*
*Voice: PASCAL*

The Professor's workshop is a forge -- chimeras are born there as swords in the blacksmith's fire. The fabrication of texts obeys the same constraint as that of blades: heat, hammer, temper, sharpen. Each maliette is a potential weapon -- a cutting parchment, a vellum honed by the game of OuLiPian rules. The monks' conspiracy had its own forge: the scriptorium where false codices were fabricated by candlelight. The fabrication of the false manuscript required ink, vellum, the copyist's hand -- the same tools as the fabrication of the true. The difference is intention: the forger hides, the Professor exhibits. The warrior's sword and the inventor's chimera are forged from the same metal -- that of constraint. The cavalry of Fontenoy carried blades hammered in Carolingian forges; the Porteur de Maliettes carries texts hammered in the Lille workshop. The game of the forge is that of the clinamen: each hammer blow deviates the form, each constraint deviates the text. The parchment emerges from the forge transformed -- raw vellum has become codex, blank paper has become chimera. The maliette is the scabbard of this literary sword. OuLiPian fabrication is a forge where the rule is the fire. The conspiracy melts in the heat of the game. The manuscript survives the tempering.
