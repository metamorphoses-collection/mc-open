#### PAGE 75 -- The Wager of Combinatorics
*Threads: WAGER, NUMBER, SWORD, PARCHMENT, GAME*
*Voice: PASCAL*

The supreme wager is combinatorial: staking on the right number in a graph of infinite possibilities. Each vertex is a choice, each edge a risk. The probability of tracing the right path in the graph of life is that of the cavalryman at Fontenoy -- the sword raised toward an enemy one cannot distinguish in the mist. The enumeration of possible choices is a calculation my Pascaline cannot accomplish. The parchment on which I inscribe my probabilities is a game: each number noted on the vellum is a stake, each calculation a wager. The mathematician's OuLiPian constraint is to play with the vertices -- arranging them, linking them, seeking the combinatorics that reveals the hidden structure. The ink of numbers does not dry like that of words: it stays alive, open to recalculation. The mathematical manuscript is an endless codex -- each reader adds edges. The clinamen of number is that fertile error producing an unexpected theorem. The cavalry of digits charges in the silence of paper. The sword of calculation cuts between true and false, probable and impossible. The game of probabilities is the most serious of games -- the rule is reason, chance is the adversary. The wager of number is believing the graph has meaning. The parchment awaits the decisive vertex.
