#### PAGE 73 -- The Codex in the Valise
*Threads: CHRONICLE, FAITH, PARCHMENT, MALIETTE, GAME*
*Voice: PROFESSOR*

The Porteur de Maliettes transports a codex in his valise -- not an original, a chimera. The chronicle he carries is not Nithard's but its extension: a potential narrative, a parchment reinvented through OuLiPian constraints. The vellum is replaced by paper, the copyist's ink by printing, but the gesture is the same. Each maliette is an act of faith in the narrative -- the belief that the text deserves to be transported, shown, shared. The game's rule is circulation: a manuscript that stays in its cabinet is a dead manuscript. The Professor's clinamen is to take the text out of the scriptorium, to set it in motion. His Lille workshop is a place of passage, not of conservation. Chimeras are born in maliettes and maliettes travel. Nithard's parchment stayed a thousand years in a library -- the Professor refuses this fate for his own texts. The Carolingian chronicle survived by miracle; the contemporary chimera will survive by circulation. The game of potential literature is a game of valises: who carries the text, who shows it, who reads it. The OuLiPian constraint produces portable codices -- manuscripts designed for the maliette, not for the shelf. The Professor's faith is the peddler's: believing the road is the best conservatory for the parchment. The vellum travels. The narrative lives.
