#### PAGE 79 -- The Graph of Battles
*Threads: CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, NUMBER, FAITH, SWORD*
*Voice: PROFESSOR*

The Porteur de Maliettes projects the graph of Carolingian battles. Fontenoy, Andernach, Brissarthe -- each red vertex marks a confrontation, each black edge a dynastic link. The enumeration of fratricidal wars is a chilling calculation: three grandsons of Charlemagne slaughtered each other with the faith of those who believe God is on their side. Nithard's chronicle is the account of this bloody combinatorics -- a graph where the sword links brothers, where the cavalry draws the edges of fratricide. Lothar's conspiracy is inscribed in the vertices: the fabrication of the dynastic lie, the fraud of broken alliances, the false oath. Nithard recounts this with the witness's faith -- not belief in God but belief in the truth of facts. The armies formed a mobile graph: vertices marched, edges tightened and snapped to the rhythm of charges. Blood was the number -- one counted the dead to measure victory. The Professor traces the last edges on the board. The conspiracy of chronicles is that each camp had its narrative, its calculation, its faith. The number of the dead does not change from one account to another. The sword is the only verifiable vertex. Battles are the most honest graph of history.
