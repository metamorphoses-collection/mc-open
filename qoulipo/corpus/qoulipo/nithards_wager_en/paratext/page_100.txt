#### PAGE 99 -- The Last Maliette
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, TONGUE, MALIETTE*
*Voice: PROFESSOR*

The Porteur de Maliettes closes the last valise. Inside, the entire narrative -- Nithard's chronicle, the monks' conspiracy, Pascal's wager, the vernacular of the Oaths, the chimeras of Lille. The maliette contains a hundred pages of invented French, a tongue forged in the constraint of the five threads. The Professor knows the wager is not won -- that the probability of being read is the last chance, the last risk. But the maliette is full, and the maliette travels. The monks' conspiracy failed because fraud does not travel -- it stays in its scriptorium, it rots in its lie. The Professor's fabrication travels because it avows its invention. Nithard's chronicle traveled because it bore the truth of battle. The vernacular of the Oaths traveled because it bore the tongue of the people. Each journey is a wager -- the stake is the encounter between text and reader, the bet is the time of writing, the hoped-for gain is understanding. The Porteur de Maliettes leaves the Lille workshop for the last time. The valise is heavy -- a hundred pages bear their weight of chimeras. The conspiracy of silence is the enemy. The chronicle is the weapon. The tongue is the path. The wager is launched. The maliette departs.
