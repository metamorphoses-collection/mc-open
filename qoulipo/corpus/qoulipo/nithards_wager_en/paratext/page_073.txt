#### PAGE 72 -- The Armed Grace
*Threads: TONGUE, NUMBER, FAITH, SWORD, MALIETTE*
*Voice: PASCAL*

Does grace carry a sword? Jansenism says God chooses His elect as a general chooses his cavalry -- by a calculation we ignore, an enumeration of which we are the blind vertices. The soldier's faith and the believer's meet in number: how many will survive the charge, how many will be saved at Judgment? The edges of the divine graph link the elect to each other -- a combinatorics of grace the vernacular cannot express. The tongue of prayer stammers before God's calculation. The armies of Fontenoy marched toward death as the faithful of Port-Royal marched toward silence. The Porteur de Maliettes sees in this analogy the material of his chimeras: the sword and prayer, the vertex and the blade, the graph and grace. In his Lille workshop, he stores warrior manuscripts beside mystical manuscripts -- maliettes make no distinction. The linguistics of the sacred is that of blood: the Latin of the Mass and the vernacular of the war cry share the same urgency. The enumeration of the dead and that of the elect obey the same impenetrable combinatorics. The cavalry charges. The prayer rises. Number decides. The Professor's maliette bears the weight of this double faith -- believing in the blade and believing in grace, counting the vertices and hoping the graph leads to heaven.
