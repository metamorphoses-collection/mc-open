#### PAGE 85 -- The Wager of the Traveling Vellum
*Threads: WAGER, TONGUE, SWORD, PARCHMENT, MALIETTE*
*Voice: NITHARD*

My parchment has traveled further than my sword. The vellum of the Historiae has traversed centuries while my blade rusted in a forgotten tomb. The wager of writing is that of the vernacular against time -- staking that the tongue will survive the armies, that the manuscript will traverse the wars. The stake was immense: a single codex, a single copy, the total risk of loss. The probability of transmission was infinitesimal -- how many Carolingian manuscripts perished in fires, sieges, pillages? Chance protected my parchment as the sword could not. The Porteur de Maliettes reproduces this wager with each journey: his valises carry texts across space as the codex carries mine across time. The Lille workshop is the starting point; the world is the destination. The linguistics of the Oaths travels in each maliette -- nascent French reproduced from copy to copy, from valise to valise. The ninth-century vellum and the twenty-first-century maliette share the same stake: entrusting the tongue to movement. The cavalry carries the sword; the copyist carries the ink; the Professor carries the maliette. Three ways of risking. Three ways of staking. The parchment is the oldest of travelers. The hoped-for gain has never changed: being read at the other end of chance.
