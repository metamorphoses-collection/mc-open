#### PAGE 67 -- The Manuscript Enumerated
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, NUMBER, PARCHMENT, MALIETTE*
*Voice: PASCAL*

I enumerate the folios of the unique codex. Each page of Nithard's manuscript is a wager -- a stake by the copyist on the duration of vellum. The chronicle survived by a probability calculation no one made: the chance that a single parchment traverses twelve centuries is infinitesimal. The number of lost manuscripts exceeds that of survivors -- the erased vertices of the literary graph form a silent majority. Each vanished edge took with it a narrative, a testimony, a calculation we will never perform. The Porteur de Maliettes has counted the existing Carolingian codices: the combinatorics of manuscript survival is that of pure chance. In his maliettes, the inventor of chimeras preserves reproductions -- copies of copies, as Nithard's manuscript is itself the copy of a lost original. The Lille workshop is a modern scriptorium where valises replace the monk's cabinets. The risk of transmission is the same: a fire, a flood, a forgetting, and the parchment disappears. The stake of the enumeration is this: how many vertices remain in the graph of Carolingian memory? The probability that Nithard's chronicle is the sole survivor of its category is high. The vellum has won its wager. The Professor's maliette carries this victory of number over oblivion.
