#### PAGE 98 -- The Final Wager
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, NUMBER, FAITH*
*Voice: PASCAL*

I make the final wager -- the one that summarizes all the others. The number of the graph's vertices is counted: a hundred pages, five threads per page, a combinatorics of five hundred thematic connections. Nithard's chronicle, the monks' conspiracy, Port-Royal's faith, the geometer's calculation -- everything converges toward this ultimate enumeration. The probability that these threads meet by chance is zero: the graph was willed, the edges were traced, the combinatorics was calculated. The wager of this text is that of the chronicle itself: staking that the narrative has meaning, that testimony is worth the risk, that faith justifies calculation. The monks' conspiracy is the counter-wager -- fabrication against truth, the false against the true, fraud against grace. The stake of these hundred pages is to show that number does not betray: each vertex is a fact, each edge a verifiable link. The belief of the chronicler and that of the geometer meet in the same graph. The probability of grace is incalculable -- but the enumeration of testimonies is possible. The final wager is that of calculated faith: believing while counting, counting while believing. The vertices hold. The edges hold. The graph is complete. The risk was immense. The gain is this text. Chance has lost.
