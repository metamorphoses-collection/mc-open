#### PAGE 96 -- The Enumeration of Graces
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, NUMBER, FAITH, MALIETTE*
*Voice: PASCAL*

Can one enumerate graces? The wager of the believing geometer is to count what God distributes without apparent calculation. Each grace is a vertex in the graph of salvation -- but the edges escape us, the divine combinatorics remains opaque. The number of moments of grace in Nithard's chronicle can perhaps be counted: the morning he survived Fontenoy, the day he took up the quill, the hour the copyist saved his manuscript. Each vertex of the manuscript survival graph is an enumerated grace. The chronicler's faith is an involuntary calculation -- he staked on duration without knowing he was praying. The Porteur de Maliettes enumerates graces in his own way: each encounter with a reader is a vertex, each shared chimera an edge. The wager of his maliettes is the same as that of my Pascaline -- counting to understand, enumerating to believe. The Lille workshop is a place of profane grace, where the combinatorics of texts produces calculated miracles. The probability that a maliette touches its reader is a risk the Professor assumes with the wagerer's faith. The stake of the enumeration is recognition: that the vertices of grace form a legible graph, that the number of blessed moments exceeds that of indifferent chances. Nithard's account is the graph of an involuntary grace. The maliette is its intended continuation.
