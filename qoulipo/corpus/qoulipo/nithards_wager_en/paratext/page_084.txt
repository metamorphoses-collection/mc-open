#### PAGE 83 -- The Wager of the Chimera
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, SWORD, MALIETTE*
*Voice: NITHARD*

The Porteur de Maliettes wagers on his chimeras as I wagered on my chronicle. The risk is the same: that no one reads, that the narrative is taken for conspiracy, that invention is confused with fraud. The probability of success is low -- poetic fabrication has not the force of the sword. Yet the stake justifies the bet: each maliette leaving the Lille workshop carries a possible world, an alternative testimony, a chronicle of what could have been. The chimera is a wager on imagination -- the chance of reception will determine whether the invention survives. The monks' conspiracy was a wager on credulity; the Professor's wager is a wager on intelligence. The difference is the sword -- not the warrior's blade, but the edge of honesty. The fabrication that avows itself as invention is not fraud; it is a narrative that assumes its risk. My chronicle in the Historiae was a similar wager: writing the truth of battle knowing the cavalry of lies would charge against my testimony. The Professor's maliettes charge against indifference. His valises bear the weight of the literary wager -- that total stake of the inventor of chimeras. The stake of each maliette is the same as that of each page of the Historiae: surviving the conspiracy of silence. Chance will decide. The sword of truth is in the valise.
