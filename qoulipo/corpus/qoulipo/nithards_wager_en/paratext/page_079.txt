#### PAGE 78 -- The Game of Predestination
*Threads: CONSPIRACY, NUMBER, FAITH, PARCHMENT, GAME*
*Voice: PASCAL*

Is predestination a conspiracy of God's? Jansenism poses this terrible question: if the elect are numbered from all eternity, then faith is a game whose rules are fixed in advance. The number of the saved is inscribed on a parchment no human will read -- a divine codex, a celestial vellum where the combinatorics of grace was calculated before creation. The vertices of the predestination graph are souls; the edges, the bonds of grace linking certain ones to salvation. The fabrication of liberty would be the true conspiracy -- the illusion that we choose, when the calculation is already made. The monks of Port-Royal prayed as if their prayer could modify the divine enumeration -- knowing it could not. The manuscript of predestination is a forgery from the standpoint of liberty, a truth from the standpoint of God. The game of grace obeys a constraint the OuLiPo would appreciate: writing a text whose ending is already written, living a life whose denouement is calculated. The clinamen of faith is to believe despite number, to pray despite calculation, to play despite the rule. The divine parchment cannot be read. The codex of salvation remains closed. The vertices hope. The edges tremble. The game continues without our knowing the rule.
