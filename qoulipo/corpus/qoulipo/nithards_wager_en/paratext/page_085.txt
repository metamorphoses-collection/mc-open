#### PAGE 84 -- The Warrior Lipogram
*Threads: CONSPIRACY, TONGUE, NUMBER, SWORD, GAME*
*Voice: NITHARD*

What happens if one removes the letter of peace? The warrior lipogram is a game no one wanted to play -- but the Carolingian tongue played it despite itself. The vernacular of war knows not the words of rest: Lothar's conspiracy is spoken in terms of swords, cavalry, armies launched. The number of bellicose words in my Historiae exceeds that of peaceful words -- the linguistic enumeration proves it. Each vertex of the narrative graph bears the weight of a blade; each edge links two episodes of blood. The combinatorics of war is a game of involuntary constraints: one does not write peace when one lives war. My narrative's clinamen is this martial deviation -- each sentence pulls toward the sword, each word toward battle. The fabrication of Lothar's conspiracy is itself a warrior narrative: dynastic fraud is spoken in the tongue of armies. The false oath is a linguistic weapon -- the vernacular betrayed by the one who swore. The calculation of forces is an enumeration the lipogram makes visible: remove the words of peace, and the text does not change. The constraint of war is the rule of my chronicle. The number of battles is the only game I know how to play. The sword does not know the lipogram -- it removes everything.
