#### PAGE 82 -- The Jansenist Chimera
*Threads: CONSPIRACY, TONGUE, FAITH, SWORD, MALIETTE*
*Voice: PASCAL*

Is Jansenism a conspiracy or a chimera? The Jesuits said so -- a conspiracy against the Church, a heretical fabrication. But Port-Royal's tongue is that of grace, not of fraud. Jansenist faith cuts like a sword: no compromise with the world, no negotiation with sin. The Provinciales I wrote are linguistic blades -- each letter a cavalry charge against casuistry. The fabrication of Jansenism into conspiracy is itself a conspiracy -- that of the Jesuits, who fabricate heresy the better to combat it. The Porteur de Maliettes sees in it a chimera within a chimera: in his Lille workshop, he stores the Provinciales beside the doubtful relics. The vernacular of religious polemic is a tongue of war -- the seventeenth-century French forged in the battle of beliefs. The Professor's maliette contains this double fabrication: the conspiracy against Port-Royal and Port-Royal's counterattack. The valises bear the weight of disputed faith. The linguistics of controversy is a double-edged sword -- the same words serve to accuse and to defend. The conspiracy of medieval monks and that of the Jesuits share the same structure: fabricating the false to destroy the true. Grace resists conspiracies. The Professor's maliette carries this resistance from Lille to the world.
