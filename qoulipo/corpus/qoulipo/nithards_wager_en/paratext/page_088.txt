#### PAGE 87 -- The Number in the Maliette
*Threads: NUMBER, FAITH, SWORD, MALIETTE, GAME*
*Voice: PROFESSOR*

The Porteur de Maliettes opens the valise of numbers. Inside, the graphs -- vertices and edges drawn on crumpled sheets, combinatorics scribbled in the margins. Enumeration is his game: counting pages, calculating connections, tracing links between texts as a general traces the lines of his cavalry. The Professor's faith is that of number -- he believes calculation reveals what reading alone does not see. Each vertex of his graphs is a page of the Nithardosaurus; each edge, a thematic thread linking two moments of the narrative. The sword traverses the graph like an axiom: the blood of Fontenoy irrigates the warrior vertices, the cavalry charges from one page to another. The game of combinatorics is that of the OuLiPian constraint: a rule -- five threads per page -- produces a number of connections that calculation can enumerate but the mind struggles to grasp. The clinamen of number is that unexpected edge linking two vertices one believed distant. The maliette contains both calculation and surprise -- the anticipated enumeration and the unforeseen link. The inventor of chimeras believes in the graph as the believer believes in God: without proof the structure exists, with faith that number does not lie. Combinatorics is a mathematical prayer. The maliette is the temple of calculation. The game continues in the vertices to come. The sword of number cuts the weak edges.
