#### PAGE 58 -- The Parchment of Fontenoy
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, SWORD, PARCHMENT*
*Voice: NITHARD*

There exists a parchment I never wrote -- the one the monks fabricated after my death. The conspiracy begins there: a vellum bearing my name, an invented chronicle, an account of Fontenoy I would never have signed. The forged manuscript describes a battle I know by the sword, not by legend. The forgers' wager is this: wagering that no one will distinguish the true from the false, that the fraudulent codex will pass for my testimony. The stake was the relics -- my bones as proof, the parchment as certificate. The fabrication required ink imitating the old, vellum simulating wear. The conspiracy's risk was total: a single scholar could unmask the fraud. The probability of success depended on chance -- how many readers would compare the forgery to my true Historiae? The cavalry of Fontenoy had charged in the mud; the monks' chronicle charged in lies. My sword spoke the true. Their parchment spoke the false. The wager of fabrication is always a loser in the long run -- time eventually separates authentic manuscripts from falsified codices. My account survived. Their conspiracy collapsed. The armed chronicler's vellum bears the mark of blood, not of fraud.
