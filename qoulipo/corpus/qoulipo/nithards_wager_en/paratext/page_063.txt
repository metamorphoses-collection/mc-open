#### PAGE 62 -- The Game of Sacred Chance
*Threads: WAGER, CHRONICLE, TONGUE, MALIETTE, GAME*
*Voice: PASCAL*

My wager is not a game -- or rather, it is the most serious game of all. Nithard's chronicle teaches me that chance does not jest: at Fontenoy, the fate of kingdoms depended on a cavalry charge. The Carolingian account is a testimony of absolute risk. I transpose this wager into the tongue: staking on God means choosing the vernacular of faith against the Latin of reason. The probability of grace is incalculable -- the stake is eternal life, the bet is one's entire existence. The Porteur de Maliettes sees in my wager an OuLiPian constraint: the rule is to believe without proof, the game is to live under this rule. In his valises, the inventor of chimeras stores texts obeying the same logic -- a clinamen of faith, a potential literature of belief. The linguistics of the wager is the Professor's: a French invented to say the unsayable. Each maliette is an organized chance, a formatting of risk. The chronicle and the game share the same matrix: a narrative constrained by reality, a tongue constrained by the rule. The Lille workshop produces chimeras as Port-Royal produces prayers -- with the rigor of calculation and the abandonment of the wager. The hoped-for gain is the same: a text that survives chance.
