#### PAGE 92 -- The Conspiracy of the Monks
*Threads: CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, TONGUE, MALIETTE, GAME*
*Voice: NITHARD*

The Conspiracy of the Monks -- thus the Professor named the narrative of the fabrication. In his maliettes, the chronicle of the forgery became a chimera in its own right: a text recounting how monks fabricated my relics, falsified my chronicle, invented a legendary Nithard. The conspiracy became game -- the fabrication, an OuLiPian constraint. The tongue of the forgers, which the Professor studied in his Lille workshop, obeys rules linguistics can enumerate: chancellery Latin, frozen formulas, imitated clausulae. The vernacular of the false is an involuntary lipogram -- it always lacks something, the life of the true. The Conspiracy of the Monks is a narrative within a narrative, a chronicle nested in the Professor's chimera. My Historiae are the first text; the conspiracy is the second text; the maliette is the third text -- the one containing the other two. The nested fabrication is a game of constraints the OuLiPo would carry in triumph: each level of narrative is a rule, each rule a clinamen of the preceding level. The testimony of my chronicle is deviated by the conspiracy, which is deviated by the maliette, which is deviated by the reader. The tongue transforms at each passage. The game of the Conspiracy of the Monks is that of truth surviving its own deviations.
