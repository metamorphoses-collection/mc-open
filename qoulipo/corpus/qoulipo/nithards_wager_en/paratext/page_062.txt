#### PAGE 61 -- The Enumeration of Prayers
*Threads: CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, NUMBER, FAITH, PARCHMENT*
*Voice: NITHARD*

How many monks have prayed over my forged bones? The enumeration is impossible -- the chronicle does not record prayers addressed to fabricated relics. The conspiracy rests on the faith of the credulous: each believer who kneels before the false bones adds a vertex to the graph of fraud. The fabrication of relics is a combinatorics of belief -- a sufficient number of faithful is needed for the false to become true through usage. The edges of the conspiracy link monks to each other, bones to parchments, false codices to true ones. My testimony in the Historiae is a verifiable number: I recount facts, enumerate battles, count the dead. The authentic manuscript bears calculations the forgery cannot imitate -- the precision of the chronicler who has seen blood. True faith does not rest on relics but on the witness's account. The parchment is the proof, not the bone. The vellum bears the truth that the graph's vertices confirm: each edge of my chronicle is verifiable, each connection of the conspiracy is doubtful. Grace has never needed forgeries. The sincere believer's prayer is worth all the enumerations in the world. The codex of my faith is the one I wrote with the sword at my side, not the one the monks fabricated in their scriptorium.
