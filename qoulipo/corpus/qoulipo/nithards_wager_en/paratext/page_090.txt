#### PAGE 89 -- The Wager of Calculated Faith
*Threads: WAGER, CONSPIRACY, NUMBER, FAITH, GAME*
*Voice: NITHARD*

My wager is that of calculated faith -- I believe in God and I enumerate the dead. The conspiracy would be to separate the two: making one believe that faith dispenses with calculation, that prayer replaces number. The monks who fabricated my false bones counted on this confusion -- the believer who prostrates before a relic does not verify the vertices of the fabrication's graph. The game of fraud rests on the naivety of faith. The conspiracy's risk is that calculation returns -- that a scholar enumerates the bones, traces the edges of falsification, reconstitutes the combinatorics of the lie. The probability that the forgery is unmasked depends on the number of verifiers: each vertex added to the graph of inquiry increases the risk for forgers. The stake of my faith is the opposite of conspiracy: I believe and I count, I pray and I enumerate. Jansenism knew it -- grace is not a game of chance but a calculation of God's. The monks of Port-Royal prayed with the rigor of the geometer. Their constraint was the rule -- that of monastic life, as precise as an OuLiPian clinamen. The fabrication of forgery is a game without faith. The wager of truth is a game with number. The combinatorics of grace demands both: believing and counting. The vertices of prayer are the edges of calculation. God's game is the only one worth the stake.
