My record is a captive song, returning endlessly. The same phrases resurface -- strife, treachery, vow -- in patterns I cannot master, a turning governed secretly by the count of passing seasons. This dance resembles a calculation laid upon parchment: six concluding phrases rearranged by rigid law, a tally of permissible arrangements that conjures the semblance of fate. My witness in these Annals submits to this discipline unknowingly.

The historian's gamble mirrors that of the songsmith: embrace the law, commit to limitation, trust that the cycling of names shall weave a fabric bearing meaning. Each leaf of my codex stands as testimony within the round -- the strands bind deeds according to sequence that the passage of years demands and arithmetic confirms. This computation of returns is the desired fruit of every historical testament: one comes always back to familiar ground, to known perfidies, to the same blades.

Those who seek patterns in verse would discern in these Annals the skeleton of ancient discipline. Hidden literature dwells in every tale that heeds a circuit -- the Frankish chronicles like the southern troubadour's cyclical song. The peril is tedium; the reward, profundity. The prepared hide folds back upon itself as do the phrases of a chant. The bound volume becomes a recurring ledger. My stake is this: that the reader shall perceive in these echoes not defect but ordinance, not accident but measure, not mere testimony but skillful trial preserved in the pigment upon sheepskin. The surfaces of my record are the fixed points of the returning song. Arithmetic sets them spinning.
