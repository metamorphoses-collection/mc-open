I wager this conspiracy shall outlast the sheepskin. The tally of corruptions wrought upon my Historiae is a reckoning that faith alone may answer. How many confederate hands have sullied these pages? How many names thread through this tapestry of deceit? The count of scribes who twisted my annals is a stake beyond mere scholarship -- it is a gamble upon truth's very essence.

The fashioning of holy tokens from my mortal remains crowns this deception. They seized my bones, parcelled them into caskets, wrought sanctity from death. The enumeration of these relies surpasses that of the folios -- each fragment tethered to devotion, each counterfeit bound to supplication. The accounting of cloistered frauds staggers the mind. The likelihood that my relics are genuine is a wager none can resolve.

The confidence I invest in my own testimony is brittle. Vellum proves faithless -- the hide crumbles, the script fades, the codex dissolves. The chronicler's hazard mirrors the faithful's: wagering upon a foundation that cannot endure. Time's confederacy is the most hushed of all -- no willful corruption, merely the erosion that transforms number into void, testimony into powder, covenant into shadow. My stake is that belief in the chronicle shall outlive the chronicle itself. The prize sought is remembrance. The hazard is the whole cloth of my history, each thread of verity wrested from the catalogue of lies.
