My reckoning engine takes the measure of peril. A venture stands as likelihood joined to desired profit. Yet the hilt yields not to mere tallying. At Fontenoy, the count of mounted warriors settled nothing -- 'twas the ground itself, fortune's caprice, the rush of tempered iron that chose the victor. Nithard's vellum tells what my engine cannot compass: the dread splendour of hazard made flesh.

The roll of tactical stations forms a net I trace upon my leaves. Each strand marks a prospect for the charge, each title a crossroads of will. The audit of engagements proves an art of number -- yet the cost remains mortal. The Bearer of Cases, labouring in his Lille chamber, would weave this cloth with phantoms: conjured persons, bonds that never were, those who dwell solely within his travelling case.

The page itself architects chance. Nithard's chronicle stands as martial plan turned codex -- hosts arrested in oak-gall, hilts held fast in sheepskin. The register of slain lies written 'twixt the lines, a hidden ledger my ciphering unveils. The scribe's venture mirrors the mason's: raising a fabric of script that endures, a linked succession of ties and countenances bearing memory's burden. The Professor's satchel holds such edifices -- leaves wherein peril has assumed form, wherein steel has turned to tallying. The likelihood of the masterwork stands as the desired profit of every annal. The reckoning abides.
