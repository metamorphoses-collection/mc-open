I wager that the Bearer of Cases keeps his workroom as one might keep a field of arms. The strange creatures born in Lille are warriors wrought of vellum, blades fashioned from syllables under duress. The Professor's conviction in his creations burns as hot as mine did at dawn before Fontenoy—that certainty that the deed merits doing, that the weapon merits unsheathing, that the travelling-satchel merits shouldering.

The workroom's sport is a manner of courteous combat. The OuLiPo's strictures serve as the Professor's commands to his host—disciplines that marshal the assault, prohibitions that guide the mounted charge of language. The peril is muteness—the creature that refuses to cohere, the contrivance that collapses like a rider struck from his saddle. The prize sought is the elegance of the manuscript, that worldly supplication which the clinamen may sometimes bestow.

Trust in this contest is a pledge without surety. The likelihood of bringing forth something new proves as fickle as the likelihood of victory in war. The Professor's blade is his quill—that keen instrument which cleaves the realm of possibility to draw forth the creature. Within his satchels, regiments of syllables stand ready for the onslaught. The hazard remains constant: staking restriction against licence, ordinance against disorder, confidence in the trial against the faithlessness of void. The workroom is a Fontenoy of parchment. The venture is letters. The throw of the dice is each creature brought to light. The Professor draws his instrument of speech and advances. The supplication of sport rises in the workroom. The portable case awaits the writing.
