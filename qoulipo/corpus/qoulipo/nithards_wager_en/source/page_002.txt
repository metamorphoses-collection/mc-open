The Bearer of Cases sets the first travelling satchel upon the seminar table and unfastens its clasps. Within lies a sheet of vellum folded four times over, a reproduction of a Carolingian blade, and a pack of cards bearing secret marks upon their reverse. He declares: all things begin with a stake. Pascal understood this truth, though Nithard had lived it without ever setting it down in words. Both devotion and the steel share one foundation -- a man hazards his mortal span upon a prize he shall never see proven in this world. The peril of holding fast to belief equals the peril of the mounted charge at Fontenoy. The genuine hazard lies concealed within the written testament, that scraped hide gnawed by the passage of years which scribes conveyed from bound volume to bound volume, from one pot of ink to the next, down through the ages.

The Professor fastens the travelling case shut. Literature of potential forms constitutes a contest of limitations, he observes, and the first limitation runs thus: acknowledge that the law comes before the composition. The OuLiPo did not devise restriction -- it merely gave it a name. Before Queneau appeared, before Perec and his vowel-shunning work, there laboured monks reproducing texts according to precepts they never thought to challenge. The clinamen, that minute departure which causes the chronicle to branch upon a different path, already dwelt in the quill-hand of the scribe who faltered against the prepared skin. The likelihood that a single leaf of scraped calfskin should endure a millennium stands at nearly nothing. Yet the annals of Nithard have persisted. Random fortune alone cannot account for such survival -- one must perceive in it a divine favor, or at minimum a wager which only conviction can properly reckon.

The hilt and the invocation, the hazard and the contest of precepts, the testimony and the trust: the Bearer of Cases arranges these tokens upon the playing field. Heaven's silence answers the silence of the ancient codex. The stake has been laid down. The desired prize is the account itself.
