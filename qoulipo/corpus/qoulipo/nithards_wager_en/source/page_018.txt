The Bearer of Cases calls forth the Nithardosaurus—that being sprung from the union of the Carolingian annalist and the OuLiPian wonder. This creature stands sentinel over tales as a keeper of hidden vaults. The brotherhood's design would have entombed it, but the measure of its returns defies all reckoning: whenever men suppose the Nithardosaurus has perished, the record of authorities unveils a fresh presence in the tapestry of its witness.

The Professor's challenge lies in rousing this being. Within the ZaZiPo gatherings, he employs the precepts of what stands neither as historical testament nor as invention—a swerve between annals and unwritten letters. The fashioning of the Nithardosaurus is a declared stratagem, an artful deception that transmutes the Carolingian account into an OuLiPian discipline. How many appellations must one summon to quicken the creature? The computation is a science of manuscripts, of strands binding the ages.

The Professor's devotion to the Nithardosaurus matches in earnestness Nithard's supplication before Fontenoy's field. The number of adherents remains scant—those who bear testimony are rare—but the ties joining them hold fast. Where the brotherhood's scheme forged sacred tokens, the Professor forges marvels. The pursuit is alike: bestowing flesh upon what dwells solely in belief. The history of the Nithardosaurus is inscribed in the quiet spaces of the original witness, in the voids of the testimony that no sum may complete. The creature's virtue is to endure every scheme. The law of this pursuit is to discover it in every manuscript, every enumeration, every forgotten pledge.
