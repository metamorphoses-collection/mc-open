I declare that every written word is a plot against the void. The playful craft of setting ink to vellum is an act of creation -- one shapes sense as one shapes a counterfeit charter, with like care for limitation, like obedience to precept. The OuLiPo merely gave name to what forgers had long practiced: the literature of possibility stands as the subtlest species of textual conspiracy. The lipogram commits theft upon the lexicon, the clinamen a knowing transgression.

The leaves of my Pensées have become a field of editorial strife. Each scholar has entered this intrigue -- rearranging, sorting, shaping a volume from my scattered bundles. The commentator's steel cuts through the skin of my drafts. The first copy is lost amid the weave of successive recensions. The reader's peril lies in seeking my true voice beneath these accumulated artifices.

The OuLiPian limitation is a willing confederacy. The law of composition is declared beforehand -- no hidden forgery, no covert manufacture. Yet the outcome remains identical: a work that would never have come forth without cunning, a possible text born of restriction. The danger in this literature of potentiality is the danger in all making -- the fabricated may prove more authentic than the genuine. The prize sought is invention alone. What hangs in the balance for the OuLiPian stratagem is the playful enterprise in its essence -- a blade of language raised against common speech, a wager that precept yields meaning, that fetters breed grace. The skin prepared for the clinamen awaits its scribe.
