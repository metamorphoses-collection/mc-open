I reckon the false writs. The count of forged instruments in our age forms a net whose chambers swell with each fresh finding. The monks' scheme is a sum -- each deceit spawns fresh bonds, each counterfeit another witness in the fabric of doubtful scrolls. The scholar's task is to number these deceptions without losing himself in the chain of titles.

The tallying of counterfeits is an art of the learned blade. Each exposed sham is steel that cleaves the weave of complicity. The brethren who fashioned false grants, relics, and bones followed an arithmetic of devotion -- the multitude of the faithful swelled the hoped-for profit of the fraud, the likelihood of exposure shrank with the cloister's might. The forger's venture is a casting of lots.

The vellum makes no distinction between treachery and verity. It receives the ink of falsehood as readily as truth -- the genuine book and the spurious share the selfsame substance. The sum of ties linking my own writing to the lineage of deceptions is a roster I decline to draw. How many suspect passages in my Reflections? How many editorial contrivances? The edge of misgiving cuts into my own leaves. The stake is that what is true outlasts the plot -- that the annals of forgery, however dizzying, cannot obliterate the hoped-for profit of honest witness. The net of frauds is vast. But the testimony of truth endures.
