That morning the professor taught them to reckon tongues. Maréchal's seminar convened in a chamber whose walls bore witness to every speech of the Carolingian realm: Frankish, Latin, the nascent Romance, Basque, Breton, the Germanic speech of the eastern marches. The master had enumerated them—seven and forty distinct voices preserved in chronicle and charter—and set his students to determine which had endured.

The chronicle of how tongues perish is writ in silence. No language falls in battle; it dies when the last master refuses the lesson. Maréchal expounded this with a scholar's precision: he inventoried the vernacular forms witnessed in ninth-century manuscripts, reckoned how many souls might have spoken each tongue, and calculated the pace of their extinction. His teaching was numerical in manner, yet the lesson spoke of loss.

Every language is a plot. Each grammar conspires to shape sound into sense, and in so doing excludes what it cannot contain. The Strasbourg Oaths survive because their chronicler chose to preserve them in the common tongue rather than render them into Latin. That choice was itself sedition against the established order—an act of linguistic rebellion whose full import Nithard may never have grasped. The master Maréchal understood it perfectly: by tallying the words of the oath, by weighing each syllable against the usage of ninth-century Romance, he proved the speech was already a language entire, not merely a dialect of some greater tongue.

The lesson did not end with the seminar's dismissal. Students gathered at the café, continuing their reckoning of Europe's dead languages, each one a chronicle of a people's determination to persist in sound. Maréchal joined them, called for coffee, and resumed his calculations: if a tongue requires at minimum a hundred speakers to sustain itself, and the common medieval village numbered three hundred souls, then every village was a school, every mother a master, every tale told at bedside a lesson in endurance.

How many languages Charlemagne himself commanded remains a matter of scholarly dispute. The chronicle claims five. The tally may well be forged.
