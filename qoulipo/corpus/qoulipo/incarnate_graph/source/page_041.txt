The inventory parchment I found in the abbey's secret archives is damning. The number of hidden swords: eighty-seven. The conspiracy is armed -- literally.

The conspirators' wager is considerable: they wager that a force of eighty-seven swords can overthrow local power. Is the number sufficient? The answer depends on the web of alliances -- each sword a warrior, each alliance a bond. The inventory parchment is the material proof of the conspiracy.

Sword number one on the list: a Carolingian francisca, two centuries old. Sword number eighty-seven: a kitchen knife sharpened into a combat blade. The number tells a story -- from noble weapon to improvised weapon, the conspiracy has opened to all ranks.

The parchment is hidden in a chest's false bottom -- a secret compartment, like those in the Professor's maliette. The number of these hiding places in the abbey: twelve, by my count. Each hiding place is a station in the conspiracy's network, each secret passage a channel.

The sword cuts the conspiracy's Gordian knots -- but this conspiracy is too dense for a single blade. The number of its ramifications exceeds my chronicler's capacity. The inventory parchment is only the visible tip of an iceberg of betrayal.

The wager of my denunciation is the most dangerous wager I have made. The number of my enemies will grow. The sword threatening me is more than metaphorical. The parchment of truth is also a parchment of danger.
