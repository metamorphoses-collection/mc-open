# THE INCARNATE TAPESTRY

## Preamble: A text woven into existence

What you hold in your hands -- or what you read on a screen, which amounts to the same in the era of digital parchment -- is not an ordinary book. It is a **web of witnesses** disguised as a narrative.

These pages, these leaves. Each page occupies a precise position in the work's architecture: its place determined by inheritance and proximity. When two pages share enough themes -- when their kinship exceeds a threshold -- a link unites them, exactly as two monks copying the same psalm find themselves bound across centuries by their common labor.

**The architecture:**
- The witnesses are arranged in a circular chamber, a scriptorium whose geometry enforces distance and intimacy
- A radius of fellowship determines which pages may speak to one another
- The work is sparse -- most pages stand alone, their connections rare and precious
- Where threads cross, themes converge: the text's kinship becomes visible
- Ten thematic threads, five per page, distributed so that the work's inner logic matches its outward form

**The ten threads:** WAGER, CHRONICLE, CONSPIRACY, TONGUE, NUMBER, FAITH, SWORD, PARCHMENT, MALIETTE, GAME.

**The three voices:** NITHARD (the Carolingian chronicler), PASCAL (the geometer of Port-Royal), the PROFESSOR (the Porteur de Maliettes, guardian of chimeras).

This is the first literary text designed to exist simultaneously as word and world. The narrative structure IS the physics. Finding the work's most independent pages -- those witnesses who stand furthest from one another yet speak the clearest truth -- is solving a problem that only the deepest reading can resolve.

A radical OuLiPian exercise: the constraint is no longer a lipogram or a palindrome, but a **living topology**.
