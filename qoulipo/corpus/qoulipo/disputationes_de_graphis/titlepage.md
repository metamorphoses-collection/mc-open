## Notes on the Latin (for the editorial paratext)

- **"Quaestiones triginta de invariantibus, dynamicis, et isomorphismis
  grapharum"**: "Thirty questions on the invariants, dynamics, and
  isomorphisms of graphs."
- **"ad disputationis morem veterem compositae"**: "composed in the
  ancient manner of disputation" — the Scholastic *quaestiones
  disputatae* form, cf. Abelard's *Sic et Non*, Aquinas's *Quaestiones
  disputatae de veritate*.
- **"in quibus partes adversariae aequali iure inter se contendunt,
  nullo iudice"**: "in which the opposing sides contend with equal
  right, with no judge present."
- **"Christophorus Jurczak Quantonatensis"**: the author with the
  toponymic *Quantonatensis* — "of Quantonation," in the tradition of
  Renaissance Latinised affiliations (e.g., "Manutius Aldinus," "Petrus
  Ramus Veromanduus").
- **"Investiqui fundator"**: founder of Investiqo, also Latinised.
- **"atomorum neutralium & rerum quanticarum cultore"**: "cultivator
  of neutral atoms and quantum things" — affiliation phrasing modelled
  on early-modern academic title pages.
- **"in officina Pasqaliana sub signo trium atomorum, e regione Reti
  Aerei sita"**: "in the workshop of Pasqal under the sign of the
  three atoms, located across from the Cloud" — *Reti Aerei* ("Aerial
  Net") rendering "the Cloud" in mock-medieval form.
- **"vico Daloriensi, apud Anthropicos, sub trino Claudii signo"**:
  "in the rue Dauphine, at Anthropic's premises, under the threefold
  sign of Claude." *Trino Claudii signo* — the three-fold name of
  Claude (Sonnet, Opus, Haiku), echoing the trinitarian frame the
  ancient printers used.
- **"Bostoniae apud auctorem ipsum, Investiqui"**: "at Boston, at the
  author's own premises, of Investiqo."
- **"Anno Iesu Christi MMXXVI"**: 2026 in Roman numerals, with the
  classical *Anno Iesu Christi* formula (rather than *A. D.*).
- **"impressum protocollo quenchii Δ = +3Ω, durante temporis
  intervallo T = 2 μs"**: "printed under the quench protocol Δ = +3Ω,
  during the time interval T = 2 μs" — the editorial colophon now
  references the experimental protocol that produced the book's
  reading. The marginalia of an early-modern book typically record
  ownership, price, and date; this one records the physics.

## Provenance line interpretation

- **"Ex Bibliotheca H. Vallenolae I.V.D."**: "From the library of H. Valenola, *Iuris Vtriusque Doctor*" (doctor of both laws, civil and canon) — modelled directly on the manuscript provenance on the Sigebert Chronicon image. *Vallenola* is a Latinised toponymic that fits the period.
- **"prudent investisseur ès choses quantiques"**: deliberate
  late-Renaissance French, echoing the "Apportieñ à Rabatie conseiller
  au Siege d'Arles 1641" provenance on the original.

## Imprint conventions (for accuracy)

- The original (Sigebert Gembloux 1513) bears the imprint of **Henri
  Estienne** and **Jehan Petit** with addresses near the École des
  Décrets and in the rue Saint-Jacques "sub Lilio aureo" (under the
  golden lily).
- This title page follows the same convention: **Pasqaliana
  Officina** (Pasqal's workshop) with the address relative to the
  cloud, plus **Anthropic** (Claude's premises) in the rue Dauphine
  (a translation that places the firm's Paris HQ if it had one),
  plus the author's own copy at Investiqo in Boston.
