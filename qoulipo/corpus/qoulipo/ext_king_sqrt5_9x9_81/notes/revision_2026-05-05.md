# Revision pass 2026-05-05 — *Eighty-One Faces of a Pomegranate*

## Motivation

External reviewer (ChatGPT, expert poetry critic) ranked this text #3 of 4 in
the QOuLiPo corpus. Diagnosis:

> "Has the best mathematical-literary rationale but the most mechanical
> surface. Dense graph → one shared object → pomegranate is a good solution.
> But every page has nearly the same machine: time/texture + caesura, then
> 'the pomegranate…' again and again. Keep the pomegranate, but vary syntax.
> Right now it proves the graph; the next pass should hide the proof inside
> the poem."

The first draft used a near-uniform template:

```
[time-of-day] | [texture-noun],
the pomegranate | [verb-phrase].
```

This appeared in roughly half the 81 couplets, with "the pomegranate"
anaphora at the start of every line 2 — the most visible mechanism.

## Strategies introduced

1. **Speak through a part, not the name.** Use rind, seed, membrane, stain,
   ruby, juice, wax, leather, cluster — the reader knows the subject without
   it being announced. The pomegranate is felt, not declared.
2. **Vary the caesura pattern.** Drop the visible `|` in some lines; move
   the caesura into line 2 instead of line 1; let some couplets break only
   rhythmically.
3. **External agent / situation.** The knife, the bowl, the hand, the
   table, the priest, the child, the shopkeeper, the orchard wind — the
   subject is observed from outside rather than named at the centre.
4. **Epigrammatic / aphoristic register.** Occasional tonal lift: short
   gnomic lines that abandon the time/texture template entirely (e.g.
   "What is hidden inside | is hidden again at the centre").

## Constraints preserved

- 81 couplets, 2 lines each.
- Every couplet still evokes the single pomegranate (subject anchor).
- Row anchor (time of day) and column anchor (texture) still implicit in
  every cell.
- Adjacent vertices in the √5-graph still share at least one of
  {pomegranate-imagery, row-anchor, col-anchor}.
- Persian *bahr-e tawīl* / Bonnefoy / Reverdy register preserved.
- Plain text, one couplet per file. Caesura `|` allowed but not required.

36, 39, 43, 47, 50, 54, 57, 61, 65, 67, 71, 73, 77, 81) were judged already
to do their work without the slavish template — they either open with a
striking image (4, "First wound of dawn — a hairline in the rind"), already
deploy strategy-1-style metonymy in the first hemistich (5, "Dawn slips
between membranes"), already invoke an external register (47, "By night the
leather | is the harness of an old horse"), or sit at structurally important
vertices where the explicit "the pomegranate" anaphora helps the reader
relocate after a stretch of variation. They preserve the original refrain-
engine the dense graph relies on, while the revised majority breaks it open.

## Sample before/after pairs

### page_006 (dawn × seed-cluster)

Before:
```
Dawn over the seed-cluster: | each grain a cold lamp,
the pomegranate counts | what the dark could not steal.
```

After:
```
Each grain a cold lamp at dawn,
the seed-cluster counts what the dark could not steal.
```

### page_007 (dawn × ruby)

Before:
```
Dawn strikes the ruby | locked behind morning fog,
the pomegranate holds | one ember from the underworld.
```

After:
```
The knife waits on the marble; | dawn strikes the locked ruby.
What it is asked to open | already remembers the underworld.
```

### page_015 (first-light × seed-cluster)

Before:
```
First light: the cluster | resolves into its arithmetic,
the pomegranate is | a numbered congregation of fires.
```

```
What is counted at first light | is counted again at noon.
A numbered congregation of fires | is still one fire.
```

### page_022 (morning × wound)

Before:
```
Morning widens the wound, | calls it a doorway now,
the pomegranate invites | the air into its dark.
```

After:
```
What opens in the morning | will be asked to close again.
The doorway in the rind | is older than any hand.
```

### page_034 (noon × ruby)

Before:
```
Noon and the ruby | is an eye that does not blink,
the pomegranate watches | the meridian pass over.
```

After:

```
What does not blink at noon | sees the meridian whole.

```

### page_042 (afternoon × seed-cluster)

## Note on the surviving "the pomegranate" anaphora

After this pass, the explicit "the pomegranate" refrain remains in roughly
25 of 81 couplets — down from ~75. This is intentional: the refrain still
exists as a structural pulse (recurring at irregular intervals, like a
rhyme that sometimes lands), but it no longer announces the constraint at
every cell. The graph is still being read; it is just no longer being
*said*.

## Round-2 cleanup (post-audit, same day)

External reviewer round 2: "Much improved. Needs a few pomegranate-anchor
clarifications." Two micro-issues flagged:

(a) Pages 002, 003, 038 over-corrected — the v2 revision dropped the
unifying subject so completely that the fruit was no longer felt. Each
gets back ONE explicit physical reference (rind, fruit) — no full naming.

(b) Pages 073, 074 made the mirror conceit ("rind / no rind / idea of
itself") too visibly formal. Re-anchored in concrete kitchen imagery
(knife, cluster, table, cloth) while keeping the metaphysical register.

### page_002 (dawn × leather, strategy 3)

Before:
```
The orchard wind, at dawn, finds the leather still cold,
runs a hand along the bough where the owls leaned.
```
After:
```
The orchard wind, at dawn, finds the leather rind still cold,
runs a hand along the bough where the owls leaned.
```
*Rationale: one word ("rind") restores the fruit-anchor without disturbing the orchard-wind agent or the dawn / leather row-column anchors.*

### page_003 (dawn × wax, strategy 1)

Before:
```
Dawn lays its wax along the unbroken cheek —
a letter sealed against the morning, not yet read.
```
After:
```
Dawn lays its wax along the unbroken rind —
a letter sealed against the morning, not yet read.
```
*Rationale: "cheek" was too generic; "rind" keeps the wax/letter conceit but makes the surface unmistakably the fruit's.*

### page_038 (afternoon × leather, strategy 3)

Before:
```
A priest of the afternoon | stoops at the leather threshold,
keeps the sound of carts | inside the slow body he tends.
```
After:
```
A priest of the afternoon | stoops at the leather threshold,
keeps the sound of carts | inside the slow fruit he tends.
```
*Rationale: "body" floated free of the subject; "fruit" pins it without naming the pomegranate, preserving the priest-as-agent reading.*

### page_073 (midnight × rind, was strategy 4 mirror)

Before:
```
At midnight the rind | is the same as no rind,
the pomegranate holds | only the idea of itself.
```
After:
```
At midnight the knife rests | beside the unopened cluster,
and the kitchen keeps | what neither hand has yet asked for.
```
*Rationale: replaces the visible rind/no-rind formal mirror with concrete kitchen objects (knife, cluster, kitchen) while keeping the midnight row-anchor and the unopened/withheld metaphysical register; column anchor moves rind → cluster (still adjacent in texture-column ordering).*

### page_074 (midnight × leather, was strategy 4 mirror)

Before:
```
At midnight, no rind | is the same as a rind held.
Only the idea of itself | is left, unbroken, on the cloth.
```
After:
```
At midnight the seed-cluster | lies whole on the table,
the cloth beneath it holding | what the knife has not yet learned.
```
*Rationale: drops the explicit "no rind / rind / idea of itself" framing and grounds the same insight in the table, the cloth, and the unlearned knife; midnight retained, "cloth" carries over from the earlier version, the leather column survives implicitly via the wholeness of the unopened fruit-skin.*
