The cavern descended through the earth in terraced shelves where ancient workers had laid bare fossil beds older than memory itself. Veins of crystal threaded through the sediment like frozen rivers, and those who ventured here marked each gallery wall with ochre to show where stone might be prised from living rock. In the deepest alcoves, limestone formations wept mineral-laden water into natural basins worn smooth by countless seasons, and the quartz that caught the firelight was sorted by those who knew its worth: some pieces destined for distant altars, others for common use.

The mineral wealth of these chambers yielded crystals that found their way into carved reliquaries, carried across the land under careful watch. Fossils of sea creatures lay pressed into the cavern walls—spiral shells and segmented forms that drew the curious, who sketched these remnants and pondered the layers of stone in which they rested. Certain alcoves where the dripping formations gathered remained forbidden to most who labored here, set aside for those who sought meaning in the patterns water traced through stone.

Where the passage opened into a vast chamber, the greatest crystal formation thrust upward from the floor in a burst of translucent mineral growth, frozen mid-fountain. The stone here bore fossil tracings so intricate that builders coveted these blocks for their decorated faces. Fragments of shattered crystal lined the pathway leading from the cavern's mouth, their faceted hearts flashing in the glow of resinous torches that marked each consecration of newly opened galleries.

The workshops near the cavern's threshold used mineral-rich clay that lent finished vessels a distinctive sheen. A shrine built at the entrance rose entirely from fossil-bearing stone, its pillars alive with the pressed shadows of vanished creatures. This cavern was prized above all others, for crystal and figured stone could purchase what force never could: the wonder of distant visitors who marveled at the sediment patterns in every mineral-inlaid offering that arrived bearing marks of authority.
