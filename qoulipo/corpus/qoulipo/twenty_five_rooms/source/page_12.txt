The forge chamber blazed with volcanic fury, its basin-hearth fed by great leather lungs that the blade-wrights worked in cycles ordained by their overlord's ceremonial reckoning. Nascent weapons glowed coal-bright upon the anvil, while the acrid breath of quenching fluid pervaded the hall where woven chronicles of fire-mountains stretched between crested shields. The encircling waters proved worthless for tempering iron—only the molten spring welling from the fortress's deepest foundations yielded metal fit for hilts inset with volcanic glass. The gate's great pivots were hammered from cinder-forged ore, heated in the basin until the substance ran fluid as liquid stone.

The armorer knew the fire-mountain as both benefactor and adversary. Razor-edged glass born of ancient eruptions, bartered from far territories where such stone emerged in plenty, matched tempered steel for piercing ringed armor. In the lord's quarters hung a chronicle depicting a mounted warrior advancing toward a smoking crater, blade lifted against a wyrm whose exhalation was pure ash-cloud, scales rendered in brimstone-yellow and molten-red thread against a field black as cooled lava. The fortress's protection depended upon the coal-filled trench—a channel of fuel and brimstone that might be kindled when the gate rose, wrapping the ramparts in a curtain of burning debris no assault tower could breach.

The blade-wrights' brotherhood swore their binding oaths above the basin-hearth, each initiate seared with a ceremonial mark heated to scorching brilliance. Their weapons carried fire-born lineage: edges hardened in mineral-laden waters, grips wound with heat-tanned hide, counterweights mounted with obsidian gems. Woven records throughout the stronghold documented every eruption across a hundred leagues, textile archives the chronicler studied when divining the next upheaval.

The gate wardens bore coal-tipped spears for darkness vigils, and the water-channel's margins were paved with heat-retaining flagstones that held the forge-basin's warmth through deepest cold. The war-leader's blade stood as the fortress's holiest relic, its spine shaped in the molten spring, its inscription channels filled with brimstone-gold. Every hanging depicted conflagration, every ceremonial rite invoked flame, and the volcanic-glass apertures of the sanctuary scattered ash-colored radiance across the assembled warriors beneath the crater-shaped vault.
