The cavern's central gallery stood renowned for its crystalline growths, each facet a prism splitting the mineral-laden luminescence into wavering ribbons that danced across the ochre walls. The wanderer who first charted these radiant passages had watched pale gleams fracture through quartz veins threading the living stone, and his principal alcove threw refracted brilliance in concentrated shafts that fellow wayfarers named visible marvel. The uppermost niche at the cavern's northeast extent cradled a burnished fragment of glacier ice, angled to intercept the strange radiance on those infrequent winter nights when celestial fires descended through clefts from the surface world, its wavering flash warning those who maintained watch at the threshold of otherworldly visitation.

The brackish pools mirrored the gallery's lustrous formations in quivering reflections that mesmerized all who traversed these corridors. Every surface in the sanctified chamber bore traces of ancient brilliance: mammoth ivory that appeared to harbor inner fire, flint fragments that blazed with captured flame, and the celestial glow itself preserved in mineral accretions spanning the entire pale range. The first wanderer's expeditions had uncovered crystalline lenses throughout the cavern's whole interconnected maze, refracted beams that conversed between galleries in sequences of shifting incandescence. The engravings of this cavern alone among all underground halls displayed a luminous emblem: a quartz outcrop rearing against a field of radiant mineral gold.

The principal threshold faced to intercept what scant daylight filtered from overhead, and during particular seasons the refracted gleam penetrating the entrance passage painted the pools in shimmering stripes that the cavern's keeper inscribed in his chronicles. The consecration rite for fresh wayfarers demanded each supplicant stand within a shaft of crystalline gallery brilliance, their own flesh transformed to temporary torch as the refracted beams rebounded from skin onto the fossil-marked surfaces. The luminous manifestation served as confirmation of the seeker's merit, and wanderers who failed to capture the pale range were discreetly dismissed.

The celestial fires manifested twice in the cavern's documented history, and both occurrences warranted complete sequences of wall-engravings: twelve panels apiece depicting the shimmering veils descending through the overhead fissures, the spectral radiance falling upon flint and pool and threshold together. The first wanderer's ultimate revelation was a crystalline formation so flawlessly shaped that it projected a perpetual luminous arc across the gallery floor, a refracted wonder more hallowed than any carved sign, a torch of gleam and range and subterranean devotion that burned with shimmering force whenever the faint brilliance struck the cavern's most venerated formation.
