# The Ochre Gallery

Beneath the fortress, the shafts plunged through strata that the stonewise elders deciphered like sacred parchment. Crystalline seams glimmered in the torchglow as wardens oversaw the quarrying of silicate from gallery walls braced with timbers ancient as the water-moat above. The dripstone colonnades in the nethermost chambers grew so resplendent that the overlord had proclaimed them a blazoned hoard, commanding weavers to render the mineral vaults with the same devotion reserved for blade-wielding hallows. Petrified spirals locked within the rock-face captivated the castle's cleric, who construed these coiled remnants as testimony to the great flood, preserved in lithified mud.

# The Fossil Niche

The fortress's prosperity flowed entirely from the hollow-stone lodes honeycombing the massif beneath its underpinnings. Warriors who had proven themselves in sworn duty received geode-crusted hilts as emblems of station, and the portcullis levies were rendered in unworked silicate by traders climbing from the gem-markets in the dale below. Hangings in the feasting chamber depicted the workings' discovery: a mounted man-at-arms tracking a bloodied hart into a hollow where calcite pillars caught his flambeam and shattered it into myriad glinting facets. The defensive water ran pellucid because the bedrock beneath the stronghold had been so thoroughly hewn that the underground flow coursed through silicate-veined conduits.

# The Brackish Threshold

The territorial writ delineated extraction privileges extending to the deepest shell-bearing stratum, and the castle's device displayed a split stone rampant upon a field of sediment-hued brown. Blade-edges were quenched in mineral-laden seepage that filtered through the dripstone alcoves, lending the iron a crystalline structure the weapon-wrights treasured. The vault beneath the banquet hall functioned as the lord's private strongroom, its silicate-crusted surfaces a geological coffer where faceted vessels and shell-mounted reliquaries shone in ceaseless lampglow.

# The Mammoth Gallery

The great bridge bore inlay of geode cross-sections that seized the dawn radiance, and the moat embankments were surfaced with shell-laden limestone hewn from the mine's elevated passages. Every textile in the fortress wove mineral imagery throughout: crystalline margins, sediment-toned grounds, dripstone frames enclosing heraldic figures. The sworn warriors descended into the depths for consecration ceremonies, genuflecting upon the fossil-paved floor beneath silicate formations the stronghold's holy man named the earth's own sanctuary—their blades catching the mineral luminescence of the castle's profoundest and most venerated possession.
