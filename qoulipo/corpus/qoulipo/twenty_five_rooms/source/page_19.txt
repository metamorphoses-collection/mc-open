The twenty-fifth gallery unfolds onto a windswept ledge where gales slice through a fissure in the limestone, bearing the tang of ochre and rains that fell when the world was young. Fronds of vanished ferns lie pressed into these rust-stained walls, testimony to epochs when tropical canopies rose above what is now hollow stone; the draught now keens through ascending passages that spiral toward distant daylight, following—so the elder cave-walkers claim—a fracture older than the peaks themselves. From the vaulted ceiling hang calcite growths like inverted groves, their crystalline branches stretching toward pools of such transparency they hold the chamber's roof in perfect reflection, as though the gallery plunged twice as deep as mortar and stone allow. In the uppermost niches, where vapour condenses from rising currents, pale segmented creatures make their home.

The climb through linking throats demands notice of stone's transmutation—the yielding limestone of lower galleries hardens into metamorphic strata that chime when touched, their compacted grain witness to the weight of vanished oceans. Water gathered in these elevated hollows carries a mineral sharpness drawn from layers the cavern's first wanderers could not name, lacking words for what dissolves unseen through rock. The draught intensifies where passages narrow between chambers, exhaling a chill that whispers of far-off surface breaches and the labyrinthine honeycomb of unmapped galleries branching beyond this travelled route. Tusks and bone splinters caught in clay beds tell of great mammoths that once sought refuge here when ice gripped the world, their colossal frames passing through these very thresholds.

Through the highest chambers, air moves in patterns that defy surface reasoning, eddying around certain formations as if in deference to their antiquity, then rushing through adjacent corridors with violence enough to set the more delicate calcite resonating at pitches felt rather than heard. Eyeless salamanders navigate by these invisible rivers, their migrations through connecting galleries more assured than any surveyor's reckoning. Moisture ascending from the pools below meets descending cold at these heights, spawning mists the elder guides interpret as mariners read clouds, foreseeing which passages will brim with flood and which remain passable. The segmented dwellers cluster where updrafts gather most strongly, their perception of flowing air surpassing any contrivance the sunlit world might forge.

The final threshold before the surface breach frames the cavern's entire descent, lower galleries swallowed in shadow while ahead the limestone fractures into granite and the unmistakable breath of open sky. Here the wind bears no trace of the depths, only the character of the world above where no mineral weeps from stone and no pallid thing crawls and the ancient sea whose crushing force shaped these galleries exists solely as conjecture for the unthinkable mass of rock that presses upon the nethermost chamber's floor.
