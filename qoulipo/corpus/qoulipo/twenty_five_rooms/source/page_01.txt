The ochre handprint pressed into the cavern wall commanded a threshold where currents bore the sharp mineral breath of subterranean salt, five fingers spread wide above a fissure where countless seasons of seepage had gnawed channels through the limestone matrix. Fourteen souls assembled each dawn in the glacial gallery when atmospheric tides shifted, straining toward reverberations that spoke of unmapped vastness penetrating beyond the constricted aperture. The elder struck flint against mammoth ivory to shape piercing implements, carving barbs that would penetrate hide with the force of sinew-bound instruments drawn from recesses no daylight wanderer could conceive.

Glacial dissolution nourished the cavern through milky percolations, bearing ochre particulate that stained the brackish catchments amber. The young crept beneath compressed ceilings where passages sprawled into branching clefts, extracting fossils with tools struck from obsidian nuclei discovered at the foundations of weathered chambers. The elder inscribed each explored passage in ochre across the gallery vault—a bestiary of fronds and coiling forms that charted routes along the ventilation currents. When mammoth aggregations withdrew deeper into the frozen plains, pressed northward by temperate shifts, the clan committed itself entirely to the cavern, constructing habitations wedged within alcoves and mortared with rendered tallow.

Flint edges abraded through mineral accumulations to open passages toward the vertiginous descent where familiar galleries terminated and uncharted void commenced. The perpetual dripping governed all rhythms. During meager seepage the clan extracted clay from desiccating basins, spreading their harvest across flat stones warmed by fleeting torch radiance. During torrential influx the draft inverted, drawing smoke back toward the glacial mouth where ice architectures fractured with detonations like surface thunder. The elder interpreted these cadences as augury, insisting the cavern preserved what the glacier had relinquished to oblivion.

Upon the most protracted darkness, the assembled clan anointed themselves in ochre and descended into the profoundest gallery bearing flint-tipped lances. They positioned themselves immersed in shadow where accustomed niches sheltered them from uncharted atmosphere, intoning toward the darkness that dilated in the remotest passages. Mammoth bone sentinels demarcated the threshold at their backs, tusks arcing upward in mimicry of the vaulted ceiling itself. The humidity assailed their painted flesh. The abyssal darkness beyond the known galleries harbored formations for which none possessed nomenclature, and the glacier articulated its primordial utterance overhead, indifferent to the mineral efflorescences crystallizing at its weeping junctures.
