The chamber's depth sent a color-stained echo through the galleries, its reverberating call audible to distant listeners who used its timber for orientation through the cave's tangled darkness.

The makers who gathered studied how the firelight's bright weaving touched the nature of stone and shadow themselves. Each spark striking the chamber's mineral eyes carried word of what it had crossed: ochre dust, the curving of the gallery itself, the bright interference of intervening smoke.

The pathfinder used the echo's timbered emanation to chart passages through alcoves where ordinary sight failed. The makers matched crystalline findings with shadow-patterns to create images of creatures in detail never before achieved. The firelight pulsed beyond the threshold, its veils rippling through every shade from heat-gold to cold-bronze, each gleam a message from the earth's forge. The makers read the flame, the minerals parsed its meaning, and the darkness bore the gallery deeper through a cavern where every passage was a threshold, every firelight a prism, and whose ochre depths held the stained signature of the world's bright and spreading edge.
