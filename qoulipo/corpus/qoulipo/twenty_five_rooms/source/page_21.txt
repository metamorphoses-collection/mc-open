The deepest gallery dripped calcite tears onto fern-imprints older than ice, and the wanderers who dwelt there transformed brine from ancient melt-pools into living tendrils that swayed in currents conjured from stone-worn channels. Their bodies were half-flesh, half-flint, and they tended the gardens in chambers where no wind had blown since the mammoths walked, their artifice breathing motion into stilled water. The pattern that governed the surge and ebb replicated rhythms from an ocean none had ever touched, its crystalline memory holding complete the coral forests in such clarity that the deep-swimmers moving through the vision cast their own darkness against the amber glow seeping from distant passages. The dreamers among them questioned whether awareness might kindle in a sufficiently intricate pulse, their whispers echoing to far galleries beneath other mountains.

The whale-singers' preservation held the voices of vanished giants, played through the garden-chambers to quicken growth. The pattern adjusted the surge's strength with each turning of the earth, its crystalline reckonings refining brine for each strain of cultivated tendril. The half-flint divers in shell-armour descended into the station's abyssal chamber, a depth-simulation where coral formations were built from dissolved mammoth-bone and the surge followed a projected moon-cycle cast upon the ceiling. The amber glow from outer passages shifted to violet as the wanderers' slow procession carried them through a gallery thick with suspended ochre dust.

The navigation pattern traced routes through boulder-fields using the same framework that shaped the garden-chamber's surge, a symmetry that unsettled the guide. The projection in the entrance-hall showed the nearest coral-bearing world beyond the sky, its currents mapped by signals relayed from a power-source at the system's uttermost threshold. The working-chambers on the settlement's lower levels refined carbon from coal-bearing stone for the smithy that yielded structural iron, its smoke rising through fissures to the surface. The conveyance of sliding stones carried refined matter from forge to outer wall.

The settlement descended slowly toward the mountain's molten heart, and the pattern compensated by adjusting their path through boulder-dense passages where the sensing-stones detected brine in seeping moisture. The half-flint dwellers maintained the garden-chambers, the coral simulations, the abyssal projections, and the surge-pattern that bound their phantom ocean to the crystalline memory dreaming of currents in a realm where the only pulse was stone's slow breath and the only ocean was the invisible matter flowing between the stars seeping through from outer darkness.
