The ochre gallery exhales its rust-breath through fissures in limestone, where the first walker's charcoal strokes still cling above the threshold. Cold drafts eddy through the passage, carrying the scent of mineral seepage and ancient clay. Upon the chamber floor, footprints lie fossilized beneath layers of calcite—human impressions pressed into yielding earth, now hardened around images of aurochs whose painted breath mingles with the shadow of hands. Flint blades cross in pigment above these figures, their edges rendered in iron oxide and manganese. The entrance niche bears crusts of flowstone that successive visitors have chipped away, season upon season, their percussion marks forming a palimpsest of arrival.

From this cavern's mouth, territorial claims ripple outward to where sight fails against the horizon. Shell-adorned walkers patrol these boundaries, following paths worn smooth through lichen-crusted stone, their routes threading between coastal outcrops. The gallery's deep-seated mineral veins forestall the ceiling collapse that plagues shallower alcoves. Driftwood beams buttress the threshold, lodged there by currents and repurposed as structural pillars against the shore's restless stone. Here the wall-markings incorporate marine forms unknown in inland sanctuaries: a whale curves across blue clay in charcoal strokes, kelp-fronds border the compositions, and the lightless abyss spreads as fields of lampblack.

Within this tradition, certain workers devote themselves exclusively to rendering underwater scenes—hunts conducted on tidal flats where the central pool spills over during storm-driven surges. Their tool handles bear wrappings of ray-skin harvested from reef shallows. Seasonal offerings accumulate in niches: sealed vessels of brine-cured fish alongside the customary tubers and roots. The entrance passage incorporates a natural channel that rises and falls with tidal rhythm, a geological feature the community venerates with totemic observance, marking its threshold with deliberate care.

Water's presence saturates every ceremonial practice. Initiates wade waist-deep into the chamber's standing pool to make their inaugural marks, pigment mixing with ripples. The discovery tableau depicts the first walker descending across a threshold strewn with desiccated kelp and coral shards. Offshore depths provision the community's feasts with species unknown to valley-dwelling groups. This coastal territory maintains marking traditions distinct from all neighboring valleys: every image bears tidal pigment, every implement has known salt-brine, and the gallery sheltering these people draws its potency from the same waters that bind this remote sanctuary to the unknowable distances beyond.
