The conspiracy of the duel is a matter of written artifice. Before steel is drawn, the conspirators inscribe their stratagems in cipher and secret correspondence—a coded record, a manuscript of deception that the devout cannot interpret. Lothar's scheme unfolded through courtly letters and sealed commissions, each participant moving through the intrigue with the precision of tournament players, every gesture a calculated risk, every document a stake in the succession's outcome.

The oath and the blade are twinned instruments. My kinsmen declared their loyalty through military commissions and sworn statements, and their actions bore the authority of armed force: every written word was guaranteed by steel, every pledge ratified by the hazard of armed encounter. The opposing faction conducted its business through different channels—through treasonous letters, through forged signatures, through calligraphic deceits that staked everything on violent resolution and came to ruin.

The soldier's conviction rests upon written authority: trust the sworn document, trust the inscribed commission, trust that loyal adherence to written orders will hold firm when blades are crossed. The adversary's purpose is to undermine such conviction—to circulate counter-declarations, to inscribe falsehoods and forgeries, to wield the quill as a cutting instrument that severs bonds of allegiance before steel can be tested.

Civil conflict unfolds through competing written authorities. Each faction produces its own corpus of secret documents; each collection carries its own claims; each claim demands its own adherence. The blade serves as final arbitrator of texts—it determines which records endure, which conspiracy succeeds, which venture is vindicated and which proves ruinous.

The triumphant party's records become the chronicle. The defeated faction's documents become subjects of deliberate obliteration. The victor's position is confirmed by force; the vanquished party's position is effaced through historical suppression. The written record and armed force are inseparable: commit your text, commit your hand, commit your conviction, and let steel render judgment.
