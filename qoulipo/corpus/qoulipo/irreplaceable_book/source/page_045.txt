The professor's maliette contained the manuscript register of the intrigue measured by steel. In his lectures he maintained that every stratagem presents itself as a problem of scribal computation—the enumeration of plotters, the catalogue of forged documents, the accounting of weapons drawn to serve hidden designs. His leather case preserved the disputational materials: each confrontation between conspirators and investigator recorded in the marginal annotations of forensic examination.

The pursuit of concealed machinations becomes an exercise in enumeration. The scholar tallied the components of subterfuge: the quantity of falsified folios, the roster of complicit copyists, the inventory of daggers that gave force to clandestine arrangements. His maliette housed these figures, and the scholastic contest unfolded across the vellum surface—each maneuver a computation, each approach a mathematical exercise.

The battle-chronicle doubles as the record of conspiracy. The plotter's dagger and the soldier's weapon prove identical instruments employed toward divergent ends, and the professor's task lay in distinguishing between them. His case contained the evidence: how many combats concealed ulterior designs, how many engagements followed secret councils, how many accounts of warfare had been fabricated to advance the victor's hidden purposes.

He conducted this scholarly exercise in the seminar with a disputant's exactitude. Every element under consideration derived from his maliette—an enumeration extracted from manuscripts, a numeral deciphered from the conspiratorial palimpsest. The classroom investigation became a disputation of responsibility: catalogue the weapons, enumerate the stratagems, inventory the falsifications, and the documentary reckoning exposes what the conspirators attempted.

The leather case held both the register of intrigue and the chronicle of steel. The professor's scholarly pursuit involved the calculation of hidden designs, conducted upon the manuscript surface using the instruments of enumeration. Every blade recorded in the codex became a figure in the disputation. Every conspiracy emerged as a sum in the investigation.
