The scholar's leather case contained evidence of every fraudulent attribution preserved in lampblack and gall. The volumes he transported were witnesses to deceit: each leaf bore the signature of its workshop, each hand revealed the intentions of its scribe. The record of falsification became a study in penmanship—the quill's pressure exposing the counterfeiter's purpose upon sheets of treachery.

The bibliographer arranged the bound volumes across the reading desk and scrutinized them beneath the lamp's illumination. The medium of the authentic codex and that of the spurious copy displayed distinct characteristics—the iron salts oxidized differently, the goose-quill left variant impressions, the prepared skin absorbed liquids at divergent velocities. Every gathering in his possession testified to deception, and learned examination deciphered the scheme.

The art of the overwritten text represents concealment through superimposition. The treated hide carries both the original scription and the later interpolation, one obscured beneath its successor. The codicologist's trunk contained instruments of discovery: Wood's lamps, pigment references, the discipline of documentary analysis that transforms the recto into an admission of subterfuge.

The history of the writing chamber becomes a study of media upon substrate, yet close inspection reveals what the annals suppress: the scribe's duplicity, the copyist's invention, the plot inscribed through nib pressure upon the calfskin of the volume. The scholar's case served as repository of proof—every bifolium a deponent, every gloss a trace, every sheet a surface awaiting the scrutiny that would unmask the fabrication.

The material evidence never deceives. The iron gall may corrode, the membrane may deteriorate, yet the testimony of the scripts endures within the bibliographer's trunk. The copyist's deception betrays itself through letterforms; the chronicle of forgery persists in the quire; and the professor's valise transports this evidence from lecture hall to special collections and return.
