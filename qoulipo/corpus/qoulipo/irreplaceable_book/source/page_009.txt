From the travelling case the scholar produced a vessel of ink. Not some ephemeral reservoir but a glass phial stoppered with brass, designed for the dipping of genuine nibs. This comprises our compact, he declared before the assembled pupils: each utterance exacts its measure of tincture. Every application of quill to leaf represents a marking one cannot recall. The spoken tongue may withdraw its pronouncements, but inscription upon vellum strikes as irreversibly as tempered steel.

He instructed those gathered to reckon their sentences with care. Not through simple enumeration of target lengths, but through deeper calculation: how many variant arrangements might this passage sustain? What total of legitimate configurations exists within given boundaries? The lexicon of bounded composition, the scholar expounded, constitutes a register cognizant of its own quantities—a mode of expression employing solely those terms permitted by statute, trusting that such limitation shall engender greater significance rather than diminished.

His case contained notebooks bearing countless stains. Folios numbered with scrupulous attention, each sheet a small venture predicated on the belief that restricted vocabulary would produce something meriting its expenditure of tincture. The master handled his pen as one handles forged metal: with exactitude, with resolution, without clemency. He permitted no erasures. He entertained no emendations. The governing principle dictated what he should transcribe, and he transcribed it faithfully, the nib recording the sum of his compliance.

Speech functions as weaponry, the scholar pronounced during instruction. Yet inscription constitutes the injury left in its wake. One pupil recorded this observation, employing the tincture their instructor had distributed, conscious that by rendering the master's statement in altered syntax, the total of possible transformations between original and copy itself constituted speculation. All rendering from one language to another represents calculation upon quantities: which utterances endure passage between differing grammars?

The case preserved everything: the stoppered vessel, the numbered leaves, the drafts wrought under linguistic constraint, the ventures successful and unsuccessful alike. The scholar transported it as a mounted officer bears his weapon—because the sum of occasions requiring it remains unknowable, and the prudence of readiness invariably justifies the burden of chalk and syllabus.
