The contrivance of the workshop extends to the membrane itself. When the religious fabricated the tomb's lettering, they employed pigment of a particular hue -- a tint that the scrutiny of material investigation would later expose as belonging to another age. Their confidence in the deception was the confidence of the adulterator: reliance upon the artifice surviving cursory examination, that none would inspect the vellum under illumination sufficiently brilliant to betray its nature.

The pen of the examiner reveals what the pen of the dissembler obscures. Every leaf in the collection stands potentially spurious, every inscription possibly fraudulent. The discipline of textual collation exposes the stratagem: place two volumes in parallel, and the medium discloses whether the hand is authentic or dissembling. The credence of the reader -- that the membrane preserves veracity -- becomes the weakness the deception exploits.

The machination of the relic commerce is a machination of medium and support. The religious counterfeited not merely osseous remains but documentation -- the register of origin, the sheet of validation, the subscription of holy veneration. Every membrane in the enterprise was drafted with the identical implement that transcribed liturgy, and the disciplines of collation could not separate the hallowed from the spurious because both employed identical medium, identical support, identical hands.

My confidence in the volume falters when I examine it with care. The lettering declares one matter; the precision of investigation declares another. The stratagem of the copying-chamber is that the transcriber's devotion and the counterfeiter's dissimulation leave identical marks in medium. The vellum remains indifferent -- it receives the stroke of the genuine and the stroke of the spurious with identical silent acquiescence.

The implement of veracity and the implement of machination are fashioned from identical plumage. The medium of the register and the medium of the counterfeit flow from identical pigment. Only the patient, pitiless investigation of learning can separate the hallowed from the contrived, the membrane of credence from the sheet of dissimulation.
