The gathering of the conspirators convened in hidden chambers, yet their script flourished through spurious documents. When armed conflict had concluded the quarrel, a fresh intrigue emerged—wrought not with steel but through penmanship. Cloistered scribes, strangers to warfare, composed narratives shaped by their benefactors' preferences rather than verity. The reader's trust—that marks upon membrane preserve authentic record—became the implement these schemers employed.

The speculum of partisan writing reveals the patron who commissioned the falsification. I have witnessed my own chronicle corrupted by copyists whose labour answered to another authority. Passages transformed, sequences rearranged, the blade of editorial machination excising whatever contradicted the sponsor's doctrine. My original inscription has perished; what endures bears the marks of those who manipulated it, and scholarly scrutiny must discern which strokes derive from my quill and which constitute fabrications wrought by alien hands.

The confidence necessary for reading any chronicle depends upon conviction that the copyists' manipulations possess boundaries—that some kernel of authenticity persists despite editorial interference, that the original author's expression remains audible beneath his revisers' alterations. This assurance falters before each speculum I raise to my own codex: certain passages I recognize, while others appear drafted in script I never composed, rendered in pigment of unfamiliar hue, and the complicity between original and subsequent versions surpasses any political cabal.

Lothar's weapon possessed integrity—it descended openly, before daylight, with observers present. The revisionist's implement lacks such honour—it operates silently within the scriptorium, witnessed only by whatever confidence the reader maintains and the devotional speculum that receives counterfeit as sanctified.

My hand records testimony. The conspirators' work constitutes revision. Between these, the ink courses, confidence wavers, the speculum fractures, and truth's edge dulls beneath pious falsification. The speculum of redemption displays solely what devout hands consent to inscribe.
