The plot at Fontenoy comprised a manuscript of refractions. Lothar arrayed his mounted troops such that the dawn cast gleams from their harness into the faces of the foot-soldiers—an interplay of burnished metal whose cost our registry of casualties would subsequently reckon. I bore my leather wallet of wax tablets throughout the engagement, for even whilst swords clashed and destriers shrieked, the annalist must tally, must inscribe the count of onsets, the span of withdrawals, the catalogue of stratagems as they unfolded.

The theatre of combat mirrors the theatre of governance. Each blow struck upon the field answers to an intrigue within the palace. Lothar's stratagem was to seize the realm by strength of arms, his broadsword the instrument of that faction. My own design—though I would not name it machination—was to ensure that the complexion of my narrative favoured Charles's cause, for I stood in his service, and the record of events is invariably composed by whichever party endures the contest.

Within my scrip I preserved the calculations: the sequence of hostilities, the arrangement of companies, the register of the slain. These notations formed the armature of the encounter, the chronicle's scaffolding. Wanting them, the account would prove mere fable. Possessing them, it became an apparatus of exactitude—a performance wherein each numeral served its office and every refraction disclosed a tactical verity.

The master would declare that one's satchel serves as the scholar's blade. You bear it into the trial—the trial of the unmarked folio—and contend with the implements within: numbered leaves, sharpened quills, the discipline of limitation that dictates which strokes to employ. The work of the copying-house is a compact against idleness, against the facile flourish of commonplace, against the corrupted testimony of unexamined composition.

The intrigue at Fontenoy concluded with a compact. The performance of refractions ended with an empire partitioned into portions: three realms, three kinsmen, three blades sheathed until the subsequent conspiracy should bare them anew. My scrip documented every particular.
