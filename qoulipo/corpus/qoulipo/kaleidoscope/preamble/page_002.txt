> **51 pages are organized into 17 groups of 3. Each group forms a triangle (K_3 clique): the three pages share identical thread sets, making them interchangeable in any MIS solution. The MIS picks exactly 1 page from each triangle, yielding 3^17 = 129,140,163 valid backbones. Rigidity rho = 0.000: no page is essential.**

### Structure

17 triptychs. Each triptych presents the same thematic material through three lenses:
- **Version A**: the WITNESS (first person, sensory, immediate)
- **Version B**: the ANALYST (third person, critical, distant)
- **Version C**: the DREAMER (second person, speculative, counterfactual)

Three voices -- Nithard, Pascal, the Professor -- rotate across groups. The reader chooses one page per triptych. Any combination is a complete reading. 129 million valid paths.
