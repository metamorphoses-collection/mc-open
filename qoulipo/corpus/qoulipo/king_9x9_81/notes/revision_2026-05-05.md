# Revision pass 2026-05-05 — *The Book of Eighty-One Squares*

External reviewer (ChatGPT, expert poetry critic) ranked this text #2 of 4 in
the QOuLiPo set and flagged two issues:

1. The diagonal-parity anchor words "silver" and "copper" appeared so often
   that the graph-coloring mechanism became visible to the reader.
2. Metadata claimed strict ABAB/ABBA, but many quatrains were really loose
   slant-rhyme quatrains.

This pass addresses both, plus light polish.

---

## Task 2 — form claim

**Decision: Option B (soften the metadata claim).**

**Reasoning.** I went through all 81 quatrains looking for true vs slant rhyme.
The number of pages with at least one slant pair is well above the 10–15
threshold the brief gave for Option A — about 35 of 81 quatrains have at least
one slant pair, mostly on the B-line (e.g. p001 plain/name, p008 feet/wheat,
p018 breath/death, p029 dust/trust, p044 bird/word, p050 breath/death, p075
back/track, p077 mint/recalibrate). Many of those slants are doing real work —
the muffled rhyme suits the persona (Hermit, Hanged Man, Death) — so rewriting
to true rhyme would damage texture in 35+ places. Honest description is the
cleaner move.

**Change:** in `metadata.json`,

  `"literary_form": "quatrain (4 lines, ABAB or ABBA)"`

becomes

  `"literary_form": "quatrain (4 lines; ABAB intent with frequent slant rhyme)"`

The same wording was also updated in `ideas.md` so the two metadata sources
agree.

**Rhyme fixes (Task 3 polish, not Option A).** Two pages were genuinely
mechanical — fixed in place:

- **p020** had `forget / miles / set / miles` (literal "miles/miles"
  repetition on B-rhyme — a real defect, not an artistic slant). Changed line
  2 ending from "their absent miles" to "their absent files", restoring an
  actual ABAB.
- **p060** had `be / breath / latitude / death` (slant on both A and B). The
  line-3 "latitude" was the weakest word in the poem; changed to "liberty",
  giving a true ABAB (be / breath / liberty / death — A-rhyme now true, B-rhyme
  still the deliberate breath/death slant which suits the Lover-at-the-Tower
  vow-and-mortality motif).

## Sample 5 before/after

### page_006 (Fool at the Tower)
**Before:** "The tower keeps a copper bell for fools."
**After:**  "The tower keeps a rust-tongued bell for fools."

### page_023 (Hermit at the Market)
**Before:** "He weighs each silver shekel they bestow."
**After:**  "He weighs each ash-bright shekel they bestow."

### page_020 (Hermit on the Road)
**Before (lines 2–4):**
  Where copper milestones list their absent miles.
  He owes the dust whatever tax is set,
  And pays in silence, calibrated, miles.
**After:**
  Where copper milestones list their absent files.
  He owes the dust whatever tax is set,
  And pays in silence, calibrated, miles.
(Fixes the miles/miles repetition; "files" picks up the bureaucratic register
already implied by "tax", "milestones list".)

### page_060 (Lover at the Tower)
**Before (lines 1, 3):** "They climb the tower not to see, to be. / The wind
concedes a private latitude;"
**After:** "They climb the tower not to see, to be. / The wind concedes a
private liberty;"

### page_034 (Empress at the Chapel)
**Before:** "She kneels; her copper rings pronounce the floor."
**After:**  "She kneels; her amber rings pronounce the floor."

## Round-2 cleanup (external reviewer, post-v2)

Reviewer verdict: "Ready after minor line fixes." Two surgical fixes:

### page_020 (Hermit on the Road)
The v2 fix had replaced "their absent miles" with "their absent files" to
break the miles/miles repetition. Reviewer flagged "absent files" as forced
(bureaucratic register imported just to find a rhyme).

**Before (line 2):**
  Where copper milestones list their absent files.
**After (line 2):**
  Where copper milestones lean past mossy stiles.

Keeps the "copper" parity anchor and the "milestones" row/col anchor; replaces
the bureaucratic "list … files" with a country-road image (a stile is a
step-over crossing on a footpath, register-appropriate to a hermit's road);
slant-rhymes stiles/miles, which sits inside the ABAB-with-frequent-slant
intent stated in the metadata.

### page_031 (Empress in the Orchard)
Reviewer flagged "photograph by laugh" as an awkward rhyme — "photograph"
was the forced word, dragged in to reach a rhyme partner for "half".

**Before (line 4):**
  The trees record the photograph by laugh.
**After (line 4):**
  The trees keep record of her every laugh.

Drops "photograph" entirely; keeps the "trees" row/col anchor and the
witnessing/recording motif; half/laugh is a familiar slant pair that no
longer feels engineered, because nothing in the line is now there only to
reach it. Anchors preserved: orchard (title), pear, knife (with the
moon-pale silver-side parity intact from v2), wasps, throne, trees.
