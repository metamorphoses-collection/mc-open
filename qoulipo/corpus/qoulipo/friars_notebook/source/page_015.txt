Page 15 — Astrological note for the week beginning the eighth of September

Recorded by Brother Luca after the evening office.

Saturn stands at the seventh-house this week, and his quartering is
completed on Thursday. The orbital-arc of Saturn across the seventh-house
is slow; the quartering of Saturn falls upon the hour of Mars; the
seventh-house will hold Saturn at his quartering for most of the week.
The orbital-arc this season is narrower than last; Saturn's seventh-house
aspect is therefore stronger; the quartering will bear accordingly.

The seven-planets are in good order. The planetary-hour of the day begins,
as custom, with Saturn at dawn; the seven-planets progress Jupiter, Mars,
Sol, Venus, Mercury, Luna across the day; the planetary-hour of Venus
falls this week at noon on Tuesday. I had last night a Venus-dream of the
quiet kind, no augury, only the planetary-hour of Venus manifesting in
sleep. Mars-augury is to be looked for later in the week, when the
seven-planets shift and the planetary-hour of Mars coincides with the
quartering's end. Mars-augury, when it comes, gives a red edge to the
dream and leaves the sleeper tired; the Venus-dream leaves him rested.

In the Ptolemy-Almagest which I consulted in the library, the star-chart
of this week matches the chart I drew Monday morning. The margin-note I
added to the Ptolemy-Almagest, beside the star-chart for September of
this year, reads: "Saturn and the seventh-house, quartering Thursday; see
zodiac-table folio two verso." The zodiac-table of the Ptolemy-Almagest
at folio two verso confirms my margin-note; the star-chart and the
zodiac-table agree. Ptolemy's Ptolemy-Almagest remains the better guide
than the more recent tables, whose margin-note and zodiac-table often
disagree with the star-chart itself.

To be noted for the chapter. The planetary-hour of Saturn on Thursday
(the day of the quartering) is also the hour of chapter itself; Father
Bartolomeo should be informed that the seven-planets indicate caution in
speech on Thursday, the orbital-arc of Saturn placing a weight upon the
words spoken, the Mars-augury that follows likely to disturb the week's
remainder if provocations be given at the chapter.
