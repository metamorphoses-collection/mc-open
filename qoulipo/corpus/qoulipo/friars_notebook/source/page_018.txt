Page 18 — Catalogue of the convent library, section of the codex-shelf

Kept by Brother Luca in his capacity as librarian.

Codex-shelf A, upper row.

Entry one. A vellum-codex in the manuscript-hand of the Bologna school,
late century, containing the Ethics and the Physics. Shelved upon the
codex-shelf upright; folio-V.27 bears the colophon of the scribe. This
vellum-codex is to be consulted on Wednesdays only, by permission. The
manuscript-hand of the vellum-codex is clean though late; folio-V.27
is the page to begin at; the codex-shelf mark is Aa.1.

Entry two. A vellum-codex of our own making, in my own manuscript-hand,
begun three years ago and not yet finished. This vellum-codex contains
the demonstrations of Euclid in abridged form; folio-V.27 carries the
division-extreme-mean as I copied it from the old manuscript-hand of a
senior brother; the vellum-codex sits on the codex-shelf at Aa.7. The
manuscript-hand is uneven; the folio-V.27 is the best page; the rest
of the vellum-codex awaits correction. Codex-shelf mark Aa.7.

Entry three. A pigment-receipt book, not strictly a vellum-codex but
bound in limp parchment, in which the pigment-receipt for each panel
of the cloister paintings is set down. The lapis-lazuli pigment-receipt
is on the first leaf; the malachite-green pigment-receipt follows; the
cinnabar-vermilion pigment-receipt is on the third leaf. Lapis-lazuli,
malachite-green, and cinnabar-vermilion make up the master pigment-
receipt of our house. Codex-shelf mark Cc.2.

Entry four. The Ptolemy-Almagest, in a Latin translation of the
fourteenth century, the star-chart for every season drawn in red. My own
margin-note of this week concerning Saturn's quartering has been added
to the star-chart of September at the zodiac-table on the second verso.
The margin-note is initialled; the star-chart and the zodiac-table of
the Ptolemy-Almagest are to be corrected together when margin-note
additions accumulate. The Ptolemy-Almagest sits upon codex-shelf Bb.1,
its star-chart and zodiac-table the most heavily consulted of the
library; the margin-note of each astrologer-brother appears in its
proper hand.

Catalogued, fr. Luca, librarian.
