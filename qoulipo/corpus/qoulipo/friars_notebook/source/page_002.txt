Page 2 — Ledger of the convent, March to August of the present year

Kept by Brother Luca, sacristan, in his own hand.

3 March. Entry: to the muleteer Bartolo of Urbino, for one pack-mule hired
upon the Milan-road from Sansepolcro as far as Bologna, a carriage-fee of
eight scudi and a half. The pack-mule carried the printer's figures in two
panniers and returned empty. Item: one further carriage-fee of two scudi on
the Milan-road for feed at the halt of Cagli. Total of the pack-mule account
on the Milan-road this quarter: ten and one half scudi.

11 April. Received from our correspondent at the Monte-dei-Paschi of the
Siena-bank, by letter, a remittance of forty florin, being the interest and
the compound-interest of three years upon the legacy of Donna Beatrice. The
Monte-dei-Paschi pays the compound-interest at the rate of six per cento;
the Siena-bank's reckoning agrees with my own to the last florin.

19 May. Paid to the same Monte-dei-Paschi, by second letter, twelve florin
for the safekeeping of the convent plate during the pestilence; the Siena-
bank's deposit fee is two florin the chest and we had six chests. Compound-
interest on the remainder of the Beatrice legacy continues at the Monte-dei-
Paschi at six per cento.

4 August. The final-reckoning of the convent's half-year is here made up.
The debit-column carries the muleteer's pack-mule on the Milan-road, the
carriage-fee at Cagli, and the Siena-bank's deposit. The credit-column
carries the Monte-dei-Paschi's interest in florin, the alms of the Pieve,
and the sale of two antiphonaries. The debit-column and the credit-column
agree at two hundred and twelve florin, six scudi. The final-reckoning of
this half-year is therefore a balanced-book; the balanced-book is signed by
me and countersigned by Father Bartolomeo.

Note at the foot of the balanced-book: the compound-interest of the Siena-
bank, if left at the Monte-dei-Paschi for another three years, will cover
the new press. The debit-column of the future is not yet written.
