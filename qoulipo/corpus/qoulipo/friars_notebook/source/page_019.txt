Page 19 — A vision, recorded on the night of the patron's feast

I kept the vigil alone and toward the second watch the vision came.

It began with the tempera-jar upon the workshop bench. The tempera-jar
was open; the egg-yolk within moved as though stirred; the binder-recipe
was already beginning; the pigment-grind of the malachite sat beside the
tempera-jar waiting. In the vision the tempera-jar was larger than it is
by day; the egg-yolk was deeper yellow than any egg-yolk I had seen; the
binder-recipe prepared itself without my hand, and the pigment-grind
lifted into the tempera-jar as though drawn by the binder-recipe itself.
The egg-yolk and the pigment-grind met in the tempera-jar and became a
paint that I did not know I had asked for.

Then the tempera-jar passed; and a gold-leaf-gilder stood at his bench,
laying gold-leaf upon a panel prepared with bole-red. The gold-leaf-
gilder's agate-burnisher moved over the gold-leaf and the bole-red
showed through where the gold-leaf thinned; then the agate-burnisher
returned, and the gold-leaf thickened, and the gilding-light came up
through the bole-red as if from beneath the panel. The gilding-light
was not the gold-leaf's; the gilding-light was older than the gold-
leaf-gilder, older than the agate-burnisher, older than the bole-red.

Then I was in the chapel, alone with my own tomb-inscription before me,
the stone not yet set but standing as if already. The tomb-inscription
had the five lines I had composed; the stone-lettering was freshly cut;
the light-beam-tomb of a sun I could not see fell through the south
window and struck the tomb-inscription at its second line. The last-
light of the vision lay upon the stone-lettering of my own name. The
light-beam-tomb held; the stone-lettering caught the last-light; the
tomb-inscription, that is, the life it summed up, was complete.

I woke from this vision at the lauds bell, with the tempera-jar and the
gold-leaf-gilder and my own tomb-inscription all present still behind
the eyes. The last-light upon the stone-lettering was the image that
remained longest. I write it down because it will otherwise dissolve.
