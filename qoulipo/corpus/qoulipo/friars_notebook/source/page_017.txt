Page 17 — Table of musical proportions, drawn up by Brother Luca

A set of intervals, with their ratios and their makings upon the monochord.

Octave                2 : 1
Fifth (the 2:3-fifth) 3 : 2
Fourth                4 : 3
Tone (interval-9:8)   9 : 8
Fifth upon fifth      9 : 4
Minor third           6 : 5

The gut-string of the monochord, stretched between its two bridges and
tuned to the fundamental, gives the 2:3-fifth when the bridge is moved to
divide the gut-string in a 2:3-fifth ratio. Pythagorean-tuning of the
monochord proceeds from this 2:3-fifth: each gut-string is tuned by ear
to the 2:3-fifth of its neighbour, and the monochord's full Pythagorean-
tuning of all intervals follows from the repeated 2:3-fifth. The
monochord is a small instrument of reason; the gut-string is its single
voice; the Pythagorean-tuning is its first catechism.

The workshop-lute lying beside the monochord takes a more complex
fingering. Upon the workshop-lute each lute-fret is placed so that,
when the sable-brush dusts its surface during cleaning, the lute-fret
pattern becomes visible as a ladder. The sable-brush was the one used
for the last varnish-lute pass; the varnish-lute phial stands on the
bench still; the workshop-lute has had three coats of the varnish-lute
over the winter. A sable-brush cleans between the lute-fret positions
without unseating them; the varnish-lute over the lute-fret must cure
at least a fortnight before the workshop-lute is played.

Upon this workshop-lute, with its lute-fret set, a small fugue-subject
has been worked through the winter. The fugue-subject is of four bars;
each counterpoint-line that answers the fugue-subject must, by the rule,
rise a fifth or fall a fourth; the interval-9:8 is used as a passing
tone and does not complete the cadence. The fugue-subject has gone
through three counterpoint-line entries so far; the fourth —

— here the table breaks off; the fugue-subject's fourth counterpoint-line
is not written; the interval-9:8 passes upward but the cadence is a
broken-cadence, cut short; the broken-cadence leaves the fugue-subject
suspended above the final; no full close has yet been found. The
broken-cadence is the honest ending of an honest failure.
