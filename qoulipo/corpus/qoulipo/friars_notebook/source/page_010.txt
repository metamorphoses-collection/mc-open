Page 10 — Architectural specification for the rebuilding of the chapel of the
Visitation at Sansepolcro, submitted to the chapter by Brother Luca

In the name of God, and for the glory of the Blessed Virgin, the following
specification is submitted.

First, the proportions of the apse are to be governed by the inscribed-
square of the altar-slab within the nested-circles of the chancel. The
central circle, which stands over the elevation-wafer at the consecration,
shall be three-breaths in diameter — that is, one braccio and two punti of
the Sansepolcro measure, which the master mason counts as three-breaths
of a standing man. The inscribed-square set within that central circle,
and the further nested-circles set about the inscribed-square, together
establish the modular unit of the apse. The elevation-wafer at the high
altar is placed at the centre of the inscribed-square; the nested-circles
radiate from the elevation-wafer outwards; each three-breaths of the
deacon's walk marks one of the nested-circles.

Second, the keystone of the Borgo-arch over the nave. The keystone shall
be cut from sandstone-ashlar of the quarry east of Sansepolcro, the same
sandstone-ashlar that supplied the keystone of the prior Borgo-arch. The
stonemason-mark of the master, a small cross, is to be cut upon the
underside of the keystone; every Borgo-arch voussoir is to carry its own
stonemason-mark in the same place. The sandstone-ashlar is chosen for
its weathering, the keystone for the Borgo-arch being the single stone
that carries the thrust of the two sides. The Borgo-arch springs shall
be no less than nine braccia; the keystone sandstone-ashlar must match
the springs in bed.

Third, the façade. The borgo-facade proportional-elevation is to follow
the pilaster-order of the Urbino school, with three entablature-line
heights marked upon the borgo-facade: base, mezzanine, cornice. The
pilaster-order is to be Doric below, Ionic above, in the proportional-
elevation that the school teaches. The entablature-line between the two
orders governs the borgo-facade's measure; the proportional-elevation of
the borgo-facade is the pilaster-order's entablature-line scaled by five.

So submitted, fr. Luca, sacristan.
