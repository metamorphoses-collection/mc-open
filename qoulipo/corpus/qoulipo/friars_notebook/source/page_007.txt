Page 7 — A dream, set down upon waking

I dreamt that a Jacob-ladder was laid from the chapel floor to a place
above the roof beams that I could not see; the Jacob-ladder had ladder-
rungs of brass and of ash, and upon each of the ladder-rungs one of the
ascending-angels stood in oneiric-step, ascending-angels without number,
and each ascending-angel passed another at every second ladder-rung in an
oneiric-step so slow it seemed not step but stillness.

I counted the ascending-angels as I had earlier counted the petals of
another figure, but upon the Jacob-ladder the oneiric-step of the
ascending-angels does not give itself to counting. Each ladder-rung was
held by two; each Jacob-ladder kept its own order; each oneiric-step
dissolved upon itself. I counted twelve and ceased.

The dream then turned, and I was upon a loggia under the night sky, where
the seven-planets were arranged in the order of the planetary-hour. It
was the planetary-hour of Venus, and the Venus-dream came accordingly:
the seven-planets moved without sound, Mars-augury lit the eastern arc,
and a voice spoke from the planetary-hour that the seven-planets were
themselves the ladder-rungs of a second Jacob-ladder, longer and of a
different metal. The Venus-dream and the Mars-augury both pressed upon
me during that planetary-hour, the seven-planets at once counselling and
threatening me.

Then I was within a figure I had drawn that afternoon: the dodecahedron-
dream, twelve-face, in which I walked and in each twelve-face of the
dodecahedron-dream I found a chamber, and in each chamber an unfinished-
vision of one of the elements, water, air, fire, earth, and in the
twelfth twelve-face the aether-fifth which Plato assigned to this solid.
The aether-fifth of the dodecahedron-dream shone pale, the unfinished-
vision within it pale also, the twelve-face luminous without source.

I woke with the dodecahedron-dream still holding me, the aether-fifth
fading last, the unfinished-vision of all twelve-face slipping at once,
the Jacob-ladder closed, the planetary-hour gone. I write this down as
one writes a ledger of what cannot be reckoned.
