Page 16 — Epitaph, composed by Brother Luca for himself, to be cut upon his
own tomb

HERE LIETH FRA LUCA, SACRISTAN OF THIS CONVENT. HE KEPT THE BALANCED-BOOK.

The final-reckoning of his accounts closed without dispute. The debit-
column carried his three small sins; the credit-column carried his longer
labours. The debit-column and the credit-column were agreed at the final-
reckoning by Father Bartolomeo himself. The balanced-book was signed, the
final-reckoning sealed, the debit-column and the credit-column ruled with
a single line at the foot. A final-reckoning so closed is no ordinary
balanced-book; this balanced-book closes a life, and the final-reckoning
is for God.

He demonstrated much and concluded little. Where his proof-margin asked
for one more Euclid-lemma to complete a demonstration-broken, the
demonstration-broken remained as he left it. The Q.E.D. beneath his last
theorem was written in hope and not in proof. The proof-margin of his
last folio carries only the half-lemma; the Euclid-lemma is unsupplied;
the Q.E.D. stands alone above the demonstration-broken. Pray for the
Q.E.D. that was not earned. Pray for the proof-margin that holds the
Euclid-lemma's absence.

The tomb-inscription upon this stone has been cut by the same mason who
cut the borgo-arch. The light-beam-tomb that falls at noon through the
narrow south window strikes this tomb-inscription on the feast of the
patron and moves down its length; the stone-lettering has been deepened
on that account, that the light-beam-tomb may catch each letter in turn.
The stone-lettering is in the old Roman hand; the tomb-inscription is
brief, five lines only; the last-light each evening leaves this stone-
lettering last of the chapel's inscriptions.

May the balanced-book be accepted; may the demonstration-broken be
forgiven; may the last-light each day find the stone-lettering of this
tomb-inscription clear, and the light-beam-tomb of noon find it clearer.
Amen.

Beneath, in a smaller hand: "The mason Marco cut the stone-lettering
according to the design; the tomb-inscription was checked by me; the
light-beam-tomb verified at the equinox."
