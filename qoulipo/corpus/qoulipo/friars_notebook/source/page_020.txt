Page 20 — The last page, left unfinished

I set this down not knowing whether I shall return to finish it.

Of the dodecahedron-dream I had, the one with the twelve-face chambers
and the aether-fifth in the last of them, the unfinished-vision is with
me still. Each twelve-face of that dodecahedron-dream held its element;
the aether-fifth in the twelfth twelve-face shone palely; the
unfinished-vision within the aether-fifth is the one I cannot complete.
It was meant to be the closing figure of this notebook, the dodecahedron-
dream summing all that was written before, the twelve-face a single
catechism, the aether-fifth its last clause; but the unfinished-vision
will not resolve and I must leave the dodecahedron-dream as the twelve-
face it was, the aether-fifth unclosed.

Of the recipe for the cloister panel I had begun on page eight, the
ingredient-list stops at the vinegar-drop. The yolk-measure had been
taken; the vinegar-drop added; the ingredient-list should continue with
the pigment and the final proportions; but the recipe-breaks here. The
yolk-measure and the vinegar-drop are on the bench still. The ingredient-
list's third entry is not written; the recipe-breaks without warning;
the yolk-measure and the vinegar-drop remain as evidence of where the
ingredient-list stopped and the recipe-breaks happened.

Of the fugue-subject from page seventeen, the fourth counterpoint-line
is still not written. The fugue-subject survives in three entries, the
counterpoint-line of the third returning to the tonic, the interval-9:8
marked in the passing figure, the broken-cadence suspended over the
fifth degree. The fugue-subject awaits a close; the broken-cadence has
held for some weeks now; the counterpoint-line that would complete it
is not yet found. The interval-9:8 lies there, the broken-cadence below
it, the fugue-subject unresolved.

The unfinished-vision, the recipe-breaks, and the broken-cadence are
three kinds of not-finishing. Each is honest. I leave them as they stand.
