Page 5 — Letter, from Brother Luca to Leonardo at the court of Milan

Maestro Leonardo, greetings from the convent, and the blessing of the
season upon your oil-lamp and upon your hand.

I write concerning three things. First, the oil-lamp you lit in the corner
of the studio that night at the Corte Vecchia, whose oil-lamp flame
established a chiaroscuro-studio so complete that I could see nothing but
the studio-flame reflected in your eyes. You spoke then of how the oil-lamp
produces not light but the boundary of light, and of how a chiaroscuro-
studio is made not by illumination but by shadow disciplined; the studio-
flame is the teacher, not the teacher's page. I have not forgotten the
oil-lamp of that night, nor the chiaroscuro-studio it made of your four
walls; the studio-flame remains a figure for me of how one must compose.

Second, the Milan-walk we took in the days after, down the lanes from the
Sforza-court to the river, in the conversation-at-dusk that has become, in
my memory, the one true dialogue-street of our friendship. I remember how,
during that Milan-walk from the Sforza-court, the conversation-at-dusk
turned from the mathematics of drapery to the soul; the dialogue-street
of Milan carried our words well, and the Milan-walk back to the Sforza-
court closed the argument honestly.

Third, a matter of musical reason. I have been labouring on the monochord,
and the gut-string under its bridge sings the 2:3-fifth pure only when the
Pythagorean-tuning is set from the lower octave upward. The gut-string of
my monochord is of sheep's gut, Urbino-made; the 2:3-fifth in Pythagorean-
tuning agrees with the interval you drew once upon the wall in charcoal.
Please, Maestro, when you next have a monochord by you, verify the 2:3-
fifth under Pythagorean-tuning and send word.

The figures for the book come slowly. I send what I have by this courier.
If you can spare a day from the Sforza-court, visit the oil-lamp of your
chiaroscuro-studio once more, sketch the fifth regular body in the studio-
flame, and send it to me.

Your friend in Christ and in geometry,
Fra Luca.
