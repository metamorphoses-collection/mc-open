Page 9 — Fragment of a sermon, preached upon the feast of the Guardian Angels

Brethren, consider the rose-window above your heads.

The rose-window, whose twelve-petal tracery was set by the masons of the
last age, is at the hour of Sext-hour the clearest of all the windows of
this church. It is at the hour of Sext-hour that I wish to speak of the
counting-the-points of it. I have myself, in a private moment, fallen to
counting-the-points of the rose-window during Sext-hour, and I tell you:
the rose-window has twelve-petal, neither eleven nor thirteen; the
counting-the-points, done six times, gives the same twelve-petal six
times. It is at Sext-hour the rose-window teaches this constancy.

Now hear a second figure, concerning the studio of our friend Maestro
Leonardo at Milan. In that chiaroscuro-studio, the oil-lamp stands in the
corner, and the studio-flame of the oil-lamp falls upon one face only.
Leonardo the painter has said that the chiaroscuro-studio is made not by
the oil-lamp's full brightness but by its discipline; the studio-flame is
half-withheld. So too is the grace of God: not the full oil-lamp of the
sun but the chiaroscuro-studio of His patient shade; we walk by the
studio-flame of Leonardo's oil-lamp, not by noon.

Hear then the third. Our blessed father Pascal of Sansepolcro — not the
Frenchman, but our own — spoke often of the probability-of-salvation as a
wager-for-soul. He reasoned thus: if the probability-of-salvation be even
the smallest part, the gain is an infinite-gain, and the stake is a
finite-stake. No prudent merchant who cast up his books as Brother Luca
would cast up ours would decline a wager-for-soul whose worst loss is
the finite-stake of this life and whose best gain is the infinite-gain of
the next. The probability-of-salvation, however small, multiplied by the
infinite-gain of the next world, is itself an infinite-gain against the
finite-stake of this. The wager-for-soul, brethren, is therefore no wager
at all, but a reckoning any ledger-keeper among you would make.
