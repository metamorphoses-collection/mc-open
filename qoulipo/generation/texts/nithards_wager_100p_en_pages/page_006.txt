-- The Professor Counts the Silences The Porteur de Maliettes opens the codex at the page of silences. In every chronicle there is what the narrative does not say -- and this silence can be enumerated. How many dead absent from Nithard's testimony? How many prayers without answer? The number of omissions forms an invisible graph whose vertices are holes in the parchment. The Professor takes a pen and draws the missing edges. The calculation of absence is a game as much as a science. The chronicler's faith is measured by what he keeps silent. Nithard says almost nothing of his prayer -- the divine silence in the Historiae is an abyss that enumeration cannot fill. The Professor understands this combinatorics of the unspoken: in his ZaZiPo workshops, in the seminars where he carries his maliettes, he has learned that the OuLiPian constraint of emptiness -- writing what is missing -- is the most fertile rule. The game consists of filling the blanks of the parchment, of inventing the absent vertices of the graph. Nithard's manuscript is a perforated codex. The vellum has degraded, the ink has faded, folios have vanished. Each lacuna is a phantom vertex in the network of the narrative. The Porteur de Maliettes enumerates these absences with the patience of a copyist: he counts the broken edges, the interrupted calculations, the unfinished beliefs. Potential literature is the game of restoring what the grace of time has destroyed. The parchment is never complete. The reader's faith fills what number cannot reach. The Professor closes the codex. The narrative continues in silence.
