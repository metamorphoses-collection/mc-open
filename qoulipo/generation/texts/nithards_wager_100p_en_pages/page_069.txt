-- The Nithardosaurus Unmasked The Porteur de Maliettes unfolds the plan of the Nithardosaurus -- that hybrid creature born of chronicle and calculation. The chimera's wager is a combinatorial challenge: assembling the vertices of the Nithardian narrative into a coherent graph, a textual monster whose every edge links one page to another. The Nithardosaurus is the inverted conspiracy -- not a fabrication that hides its lie, but an invention that exhibits its method. The number of its vertices equals the number of pages: a hundred nodes, hundreds of thematic edges. The enumeration of the beast is a calculation the Professor performs in his Lille workshop, surrounded by his maliettes opened like organs on an anatomy table. The chimera bears Nithard's name because the Carolingian chronicle is its skeleton -- the Historiae provide the bones that the monks' conspiracy had sought to falsify. The Nithardosaurus's risk is that of every invention: that no one recognize the beast, that the combinatorics remain illegible. The maliette's stake is the same as the manuscript's: to be opened, to be read, to be understood. The probability that the chimera survives depends on chance -- how many readers will dare enumerate its vertices? The Professor closes his valises. The Nithardosaurus's wager is launched. The graph lives.
