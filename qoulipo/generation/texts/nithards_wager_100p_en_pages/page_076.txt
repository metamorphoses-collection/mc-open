-- The Conspiracy of Numbers The monks counted too -- but their numbers lied. The relic conspiracy is a game of falsified enumeration: attributing bones, multiplying forgeries, calculating the number of credulous needed for the fraud to take. The fabrication of faith is a combinatorics of the lie -- each false vertex added to the graph of saints requires new edges, new proofs, new calculations. The Porteur de Maliettes demonstrated it in his seminar: the monks' conspiracy is a graph that collapses under its own weight. Too many phantom vertices, too many edges without substance. The faithful's belief kept the graph alive -- but sincere faith cannot indefinitely sustain a falsified number. In the Professor's maliettes, in Lille, chimeras bear honest numbers: the calculation of invention does not cheat with vertices. The game of potential literature is a transparent conspiracy -- an avowed fabrication, a displayed constraint. The OuLiPo's rule is the inverse of the forgers': showing the calculation, revealing the combinatorics, avowing the number. The monk's clinamen is to cheat; the Professor's is to play fair. My chronicler's faith demands true numbers. The maliette containing the true graph weighs more than the one containing the false. God's enumeration never lies.
