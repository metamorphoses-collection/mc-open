-- The Belief Carried I wager that the Porteur de Maliettes believes without knowing it. His manner of carrying texts from workshop to seminar, from Lille to ZaZiPo, is a form of faith -- that belief that the chronicle deserves the journey, that the narrative is worth being played before an audience. His game is mine: staking on the invisible. The hoped-for gain is not certainty but the grace of shared doubt. Nithard's chronicle sleeps in the Professor's maliettes as the Pensees sleep in my papers. The Carolingian warrior's testimony and the Jansenist's prayer share the same stake: saying something true in a world that prefers silence. Chance willed that our texts survive -- infinitesimal probability, immense gain. The game of potential literature consists of multiplying the rules to multiply the chances of saying what is true. Each constraint is a prayer. Each clinamen, a grace. The Professor carries the faith of others. In his maliettes, Nithard's chimeras share space with my calculations. The wager is always the same: staking on the narrative against divine silence, against oblivion, against time. The risk is that of incomprehension -- who still reads Carolingian chronicles? who still plays the game of OuLiPian constraint? But the Professor's belief is more stubborn than chance. He stakes his life on these maliettes as I stake mine on the hoped-for gain of eternity. The game has no end. The chronicle is replayed at each seminar. Faith is carried as one carries a valise -- with obstinacy, with grace, without guarantee.
