-- The Ambulant Parchment The Porteur de Maliettes is an ambulant parchment. He carries the chronicle in his body -- the testimony of Nithard and Pascal's wager inscribed in his gait, in the tongue he employs, in the game of his interventions. The Professor's vernacular is a contemporary French worked by constraint, a literary tongue that knows it descends from the Romance of the Oaths. The wager of the living chronicle is his. Staking that the narrative deserves movement, that the parchment must not remain in libraries -- this is the Professor's stake. The probability that someone will listen is uncertain; the chance of audiences is capricious. But the stake is placed at each seminar: the Professor opens his maliette, deploys his chimeras, plays the game of potential literature before listeners whom linguistics does not always interest. The hoped-for gain is transmission. Nithard's manuscript lives only if someone carries it -- and the Professor's game consists of finding, in each era, the tongue that will revive the chronicle. The codex does not change, but the vernacular commenting on it evolves. The OuLiPo gave the Professor a rule: every constraint is a reading key, every clinamen a door to meaning. The parchment is a game replayed at each reading. The chronicle is a wager relaunched in each era. The Professor is risk incarnate -- the one who stakes his voice on the vellum of others, his tongue on the ink of the dead, his game on the chronicle of the past. The parchment travels. The tongue adapts. The wager continues.
