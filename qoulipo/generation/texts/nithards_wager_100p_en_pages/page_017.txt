-- The Nithardosaurus Watches The Porteur de Maliettes invokes the Nithardosaurus -- that creature born from the crossing of the Carolingian chronicler and the OuLiPian beast. The monster watches over narratives like a guardian of the crypt. The monks' conspiracy sought to bury it, but the number of its resurgences is a calculation that defies faith: each time one believes the Nithardosaurus has vanished, the enumeration of sources reveals a new vertex in the graph of its testimony. The Professor's game consists of awakening the beast. In the ZaZiPo seminars, he deploys the rules of what is neither chronicle nor fiction -- a clinamen between history and potential literature. The fabrication of the Nithardosaurus is an avowed conspiracy, a creative fraud that transforms the Carolingian narrative into an OuLiPian constraint. How many vertices does it take to bring the monster to life? The calculation is a combinatorics of texts, of edges linking the centuries. The Professor's faith in the Nithardosaurus is as sincere as Nithard's prayer before Fontenoy. The number of believers is limited -- the vertices of the graph are few -- but the edges linking them are solid. The monks' conspiracy fabricated relics; the Professor fabricates chimeras. The game is the same: giving body to what exists only in belief. The chronicle of the Nithardosaurus is written in the silences of the original testimony, in the blanks of the account that enumeration cannot fill. The monster's grace is to survive every conspiracy. The game's rule is to seek it in each codex, each calculation, each forgotten prayer.
