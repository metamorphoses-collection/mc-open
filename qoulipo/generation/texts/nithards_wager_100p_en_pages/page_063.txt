-- The Vertices of Fraud The conspiracy is a game of numbers. Each forged parchment is a vertex in the graph of fabrication -- the edges link the forgers to each other, the monks to the relic merchants, the bones to the falsified codices. The enumeration of medieval frauds is a vertiginous calculation: how many manuscripts are false? How many vellums bear mendacious ink? The combinatorics of deception obeys rules the OuLiPo would recognize -- any constraint can produce the true as well as the false. Nithard's sword is a fact; his parchment could be a game. Potential literature admits the false as a combinatorial possibility: one more vertex in the graph of possible texts. The forger's clinamen is that deviation transforming the copy into fabrication. Cavalry and conspiracy share the same calculation: enumerating forces before engaging battle. Armies count blades; monks count vellums. Each edge of the fraudulent graph bears the weight of a lie. The number of forgeries may surpass that of the true -- the war of manuscripts is as bloody as that of swords. The game of fabrication demands a rule: imitate without being caught. The parchment is the battlefield. The graph's vertices are the dead of truth.
