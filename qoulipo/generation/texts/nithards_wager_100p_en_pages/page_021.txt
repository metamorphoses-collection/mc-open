-- The Conspiracy of the Game I wager that every text is a conspiracy against silence. The game of writing is a fabrication -- one fabricates meaning as one fabricates a forged parchment, with the same attention to constraint, the same submission to the rule. The OuLiPo merely named what forgers already practiced: potential literature is the most refined form of textual conspiracy. The lipogram is a fraud upon the dictionary, the clinamen a complicit deviation. The manuscript of my Pensees is an editorial battlefield. Each editor has played the game of conspiracy -- recomposing, ordering, fabricating a codex from my disordered bundles. The critic's sword cuts into the vellum of my drafts. The original parchment is lost in the graph of successive editions. The reader's wager is to find my voice beneath the layers of fabrication. The OuLiPian constraint is a joyful conspiracy. The rule of the game is announced in advance -- no secret fraud, no clandestine fabrication. But the result is the same: a text that would not have existed without artifice, a potential parchment born of constraint. The risk of potential literature is that of all fabrication -- the false can become truer than the true. The hoped-for gain is invention itself. The stake of the OuLiPian conspiracy is the game as such -- a sword of words brandished against ordinary prose, a wager that the rule produces meaning, that constraint begets beauty. The parchment of the clinamen awaits its copyist.
