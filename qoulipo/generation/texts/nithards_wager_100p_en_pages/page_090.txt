-- The Vernacular of Betrayal Lothar betrayed in every tongue -- in Latin before the clerks, in vernacular before the warriors. The dynastic conspiracy is a linguistic game: choosing the tongue of the lie, adapting the fraud to the ear of the one being deceived. The nascent French of the Oaths is the tongue of loyalty -- Louis and Charles swear in the words of the people because political faith demands transparency. The sword of betrayal and the sword of fidelity speak different tongues. Lothar's cavalry charged while lying; that of my kings charged while swearing. The fabrication of the false oath is the oldest of linguistic conspiracies: promising in one tongue what one will betray in another. The game of the given word is a constraint the OuLiPo would understand -- the rule is to speak true, the clinamen is to lie. The minimal deviation between oath and betrayal is a word -- a single vernacular mispronounced. The warrior's faith who swears is the player's who stakes: the risk is death, the stake is honor. Lothar's conspiracy failed because the tongue of betrayal always ends by betraying itself. The sword of truth is the French of the Oaths -- the vernacular that survived the lie. The constraint of loyalty is the only game rule that holds. Conspiracy is a lipogram of honor.
