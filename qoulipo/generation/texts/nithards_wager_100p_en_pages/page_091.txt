-- The Professor's Clinamen The Porteur de Maliettes is a living clinamen -- a minimal deviation in the course of French letters. His wager is that of linguistic invention: fabricating a tongue within the tongue, a French within French, a chimeric vernacular. The maliette is his tool -- each valise carries a deviated text, an assumed constraint, a game of rules where the conspiracy of the real transforms into potential literature. The inventor of chimeras does not fabricate forgeries -- he fabricates possibilities. The monks' fraud was a conspiracy against truth; the Professor's clinamen is a conspiracy against the banal. In his Lille workshop, fabrication obeys the OuLiPian rule: each constraint produces a text that freedom would not have invented. The Professor's lipogram is to subtract the ordinary -- to remove from French what goes without saying to reveal what surprises. The tongue of his maliettes is a vernacular of the possible -- a French deviated just enough to hear the rule beneath the words. The wager is risky: who will read the Professor's chimeras? The stake is the survival of the game -- that constraint continue to produce meaning, that the clinamen never cease to deviate. The probability of literary success is low. The chance of readings will decide. But the maliette travels. The fabrication continues. The conspiracy of beauty is the only one worth the stake.
