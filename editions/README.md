Each edition folder holds the current text (as published on the website today):
- `<edition>.tei.xml` — **TEI P5**: header (source, copy, conventions, responsibility, licence), facsimile (IIIF canvas
  per page, a zone per verse), the Latin text and the English translation as a `<group>` of two texts; every English
  line points to its Latin verse (`@corresp`); Virgil source tags are margin notes with `<ref>` to CTS URNs.
  Generated by `tools/tei/tei_export.py` from `edition.json`.
- `edition.json` — the full edition model used by the viewer (text, translation, loci, scansion, line boxes, notes).
- `book.json`, `book.txt` — clean per-line records and plain text; `manifest.json` — IIIF Presentation 3.

## Conventions
Diplomatic transcription of the collection copy: spelling, u/v, i/j, ligatures, abbreviations and accents as printed
(the Ross text expands ligatures and abbreviations: æ → ae, tilde → m/n); long s → s. Printer's misprints are kept as
printed and noted. Pages are named by the collation of the copy ([i]–[iv], p. 1, …). The English faces the Latin line by
line; a word may sit across the line break where the sense of the pair is kept.

## How the current text was established (Sept. 2026)
**English.** Two independent full retranslations (Gemini 3.1 Pro, Claude Opus 5.5) and a blind referee (Grok 4.7) with
planted control items; triage of every flag against the Virgil source; corrections polished and blind-verified; the
residue ruled by the editor. Capilupi: 116 lines corrected; Ross: 1,001 + 79 + 13 lines (675 lines had carried English
from another passage, an assembly defect, now retranslated).

**Latin.** Two blind machine readings of every verse from native-resolution crops (a third reader on disputed Ross
lines), compared letter by letter with the transcription; divergences adjudicated against the image by reader agents
with hidden controls; changes checked by eye; Ross corrections collated with a second exemplar (Internet Archive copy).
Capilupi: 8 lines; Ross: 643 lines (mostly ligatures and nasal tildes dropped by the original OCR, turnover and turn-up
completions, misreadings).

**Line boxes.** Rebuilt from the print (Capilupi 132; Ross 10,373 incl. 2,009 turnover lines).

## Tools
`tools/tei/` TEI generator · `tools/translation_check/` translation check pipeline · `tools/latin_pass/` Latin pass
pipeline (crops, readers, comparison, adjudication packets, application with guards). They call models through
OpenRouter (`OPENROUTER_API_KEY` from the environment) and read the edition data from this repository's `editions/`
folder (set `MC_EDITIONS` to point elsewhere). Page images come from the IIIF manifests.
